AI transcription turns your recorded calls into text automatically. You can read each call as a conversation, search what was said, get summaries, and spot calls that need a supervisor. This page is for admins who set it up and manage billing.

<Note>
  AI transcription is available on the **Growth** and **Professional** plans, including trials. Only admins can change these settings.
</Note>

## What you get

When AI transcription is on for a member, their recorded calls are transcribed after each call ends. You then get:

- **Transcripts** in **Reports**, shown as a conversation with speaker labels. See [Transcripts and summaries](/ai/transcripts-and-summaries).
- **Call search** across what was said, by exact words or by meaning. See [Call search](/ai/call-search).
- **Escalations** for calls that need a supervisor. See [Escalations](/ai/escalations).
- **AI insights** dashboards with sentiment, resolution, and scorecards. See [AI insights](/ai/ai-insights).

## Pricing

AI transcription is a per-member add-on.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Add-on price | $10 per member per month, or $120 per member per year on yearly plans |
| Included minutes | 1,500 minutes per member each month |
| Beyond the included minutes | $0.01 per minute, charged from your wallet |
| First month | The fee is prorated from the day you turn it on. The full 1,500 minutes apply right away. |
| Short calls | Calls shorter than your minimum call duration are skipped and never charged |
| Internal calls | Calls between your own members are never transcribed |

Calls are transcribed from the moment you turn it on. Earlier calls are not transcribed.

<Tip>
  Included minutes belong to each member. They are not pooled across your company.
</Tip>

## Before you start

You need two things before you can turn AI transcription on:

1. Your company is on the **Growth** or **Professional** plan. If not, the page shows **View plans**.
2. **Auto Top-up** is on for your wallet. Overage minutes are charged from the wallet, so TalkChief asks for it first. If it is off, saving shows "Turn on auto top-up to use AI transcription".

<Warning>
  While AI transcription is on, you cannot turn off auto top-up. Turn off AI transcription first.
</Warning>

## How the settings work

You can set AI transcription at three levels. The most specific level wins.

| Level | Where | Options |
|---|---|---|
| Company | **Settings** > **AI transcription** | On or Off |
| Team | **Teams & Members** > **Manage Team** > **Settings** tab | **Inherit**, **On**, or **Off** |
| Member | **Teams & Members** > member **Settings** | **Inherit**, **On**, or **Off** |

- **Inherit** follows the level above. A member follows their team. A team follows the company.
- A team or member set to **On** or **Off** overrides the level above.
- The company setting applies to every current and future member, unless a team or member setting says otherwise.

When you turn a level on, you also choose:

- **Call types**: **Inbound**, **Outbound**, and **Queues**. Pick at least one.
- **Minimum call duration (seconds)**: 0 to 600. Shorter calls are skipped and never charged. The default is 10 seconds.

## Turn on AI transcription for your company

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the settings">
    Go to **Settings** > **AI transcription**. The card shows the pricing for your plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch it on">
    Turn on **Transcribe calls for the whole company**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to transcribe">
    Under **Call types**, select **Inbound**, **Outbound**, **Queues**, or any mix. Set **Minimum call duration (seconds)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and confirm">
    Select **Save changes**. The **Turn on AI transcription?** dialog shows how many members the add-on covers and the cost. Select **Turn on and pay**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The rest of the current billing period is charged now, prorated. A message shows the amount charged.

## Set AI transcription for a team or member

Use a team or member setting to cover only some people, or to exclude someone.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the team or member">
    Go to **Teams & Members**. For a team, select **Manage Team**, then open the **Settings** tab. The team opens on **Members** first. For a member, open the member's **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the AI transcription card">
    Scroll to the **AI transcription** card. It is saved on its own, separate from the rest of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a mode">
    Select **Inherit**, **On**, or **Off**. With **Inherit**, the card shows what applies, for example "Inherited from company: On (Inbound, Queues; calls of 10s or longer)".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    If you chose **On**, set **Call types** and **Minimum call duration (seconds)**. Select **Save changes**, then confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

On a member's card, **In effect now** shows the setting that applies to that member today, and where it comes from.

<Frame>
  ![Member settings page, AI transcription card with the Inherit / On / Off control, 'In effect now' line and an Active status badge](/images/docs/ai-transcription-member-card.png)
</Frame>

<Tip>
  To transcribe only one team, keep the company **Off** and set that team to **On**. To exclude one person from a company-wide setting, set that member to **Off**.
</Tip>

## Check usage

**Settings** > **AI transcription** > **Usage this month** lists each member with AI transcription:

- **Minutes**: minutes used of the minutes included this month, for example "320 of 1,500 min".
- **Status**: **Active**, or **Active until** a date **(not renewing)** after you turn it off.

</Frame>

## Turn AI transcription off

Set the company switch to off, or set a team or member to **Off**, then select **Save changes** and **Turn off**.

- Coverage stays active until the end of the paid period. Then it stops renewing.
- The paid period is not refunded.
- Calls during the remaining paid period are still transcribed.

To change your mind before the period ends, select **Undo** next to the member in **Usage this month**. Nothing is charged again, because the period is already paid.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The switch is disabled or I see a plan notice">
    AI transcription needs the Growth or Professional plan. Select **View plans** to upgrade.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Saving fails with a payment message">
    Turn on **Auto Top-up** for your wallet, or update your card if a payment failed. The message links to **View plans** and **Open wallet**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A call was not transcribed">
    Check that the member was covered when the call happened, that its call type was selected, and that the call was longer than the minimum call duration. Internal calls between members and calls from before you turned it on are never transcribed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I see &quot;Only admins can manage AI transcription&quot;">
    Only admins can view and change these settings. Ask an admin on your account.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do unused minutes roll over?">
    No. Each member gets 1,500 included minutes each month.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How are overage minutes charged?">
    At $0.01 per minute from your wallet, prorated per second.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I transcribe calls from last week?">
    No. Calls are transcribed only from the moment AI transcription is turned on for that member.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there an API for transcribing my own audio files?">
    Yes, the Transcription API is a separate product with its own pricing. See the [Transcription API](/developers/transcription-api).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
