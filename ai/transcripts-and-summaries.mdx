Every transcribed call has a transcript you can read as a conversation. You can play the recording from any line and generate a summary with key points and action items. This page is for admins and supervisors who review calls.

<Note>
  Transcripts exist only for calls covered by [AI transcription](/ai/ai-transcription). Admins see every call in the company. Supervisors see calls from their own teams.
</Note>

## Open a transcript

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Reports">
    Go to **Insights** > **Reports** and pick the **Outbound**, **Inbound**, or **Queue** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the call">
    Use the filters to narrow the list, then select **Apply**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the transcript">
    In the **RECORDING** column, select the **Open transcript** icon next to the recording player. Calls without the icon have no transcript.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **Call transcript** drawer opens at the side of the page. Its header shows the call date and time, the call type, the languages spoken, and the call length.

<Frame>
  ![Insights > Reports, Inbound tab, a row's RECORDING cell with the play button, the Open transcript icon and a red High escalation badge](/images/docs/transcript-open-from-reports.png)
</Frame>

You can also open transcripts from [Call search](/ai/call-search) and [Escalations](/ai/escalations).

## Read the conversation

The **Conversation** tab shows the call as chat bubbles, one per turn. Each bubble shows who spoke, what they said, and when.

Speaker labels work like this:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| The member's name | Your team member on the call. Their bubbles sit on the right. |
| **Customer** | The other party on the call. Their bubbles sit on the left. |
| **Speaker 1**, **Speaker 2** | Shown when TalkChief cannot tell which side is which |

- Select any bubble to play the recording from that point.
- Use the player at the top of the drawer to play or pause the recording.

If the member and the customer look reversed, select **Swap sides**. This only changes the view. It does not change the transcript.

</Frame>

## Insight chips

Chips at the top of the drawer summarize the call:

- **Sentiment**: the customer's overall mood, from **Very negative** to **Very positive**.
- **Intent**: why the customer called, for example **Support request**, **Billing**, or **Cancellation**.
- **Resolution**: **Resolved**, **Not resolved**, or **Unclear**.
- **Churn risk** and **Complaint** appear when the call shows those signals.
- **Uncertain** appears when the result is not reliable enough to show.

When a call is escalated, a red **Escalation** chip shows its severity. Select it to jump to the line where the customer asked to escalate. That line is highlighted. See [Escalations](/ai/escalations).

## Generate a summary

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Summary tab">
    In the transcript drawer, select **Summary**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate it">
    If the call has no summary yet, select **Generate summary**. It takes a few seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review it">
    Read the short summary, then the **Key points** and **Action items**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Escalated calls get a summary automatically, so it is ready when a supervisor opens the call.

<Frame>
  ![Call transcript drawer, Summary tab with a short summary paragraph, a Key points list and an Action items list](/images/docs/transcript-drawer-summary.png)
</Frame>

<Warning>
  Transcripts, insights, and summaries are generated automatically and can contain mistakes. Check the recording before you use them for compliance, legal, or performance decisions.
</Warning>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="There is no transcript icon on a call">
    The call was not transcribed. It may be shorter than the minimum call duration, of a call type you did not select, internal, or from before AI transcription was turned on. Transcription also takes a few minutes after the call ends.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="&quot;No speech was found in this call&quot;">
    The recording had no speech to transcribe, for example a silent or very short call.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="&quot;The summary could not be generated&quot;">
    A failed summary can't be generated again from the drawer. Contact TalkChief support and include the call's date, time, and phone number.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which languages are supported?">
    Transcripts show each line in the language it was spoken. Calls that switch languages show more than one language in the drawer header.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can agents see transcripts?">
    No. Transcripts are available to admins and to supervisors for their own teams.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can summaries be posted to CoWork?">
    Escalated calls can be posted to CoWork channels with their summary. See [Escalations](/ai/escalations).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
