TalkChief for Bitrix24 logs every TalkChief phone call in your Bitrix24 portal without anyone typing it in. You connect once with your portal address and your Bitrix24 login, with no webhooks or keys to copy, and from then on each finished call is saved in Bitrix24's telephony and on the matching CRM record, with its result, talk time, the agent who handled it and the recording. It works with Bitrix24 cloud portals and with self-hosted (on-premise) Bitrix24.

## What the integration does

- **Logs every external call**: inbound and outbound calls are saved through Bitrix24's own telephony, so they appear in Bitrix24's call statistics and as a call activity on the CRM record. Calls between two TalkChief extensions are not logged.
- **Finds the right record**: Bitrix24 matches the caller's number (or the number you dialed) to a contact, company or lead in your CRM.
- **Adds new callers to your CRM, if you want**: with **Create leads for new numbers** on, a number no record has becomes a new **lead**. If your Bitrix24 CRM works without leads (simple mode), TalkChief adds a new **contact** instead. It is off for new connections.
- **Logs the call under the agent**: the call belongs to the Bitrix24 user whose email matches the TalkChief agent's email. When no user matches, it belongs to the responsible user of the record that has the number, and otherwise to the Bitrix24 user who connected TalkChief. New leads and contacts are assigned to the same user.
- **Adds the recording**: when the call was recorded, Bitrix24 downloads the recording from TalkChief and attaches it to the call and its activity. TalkChief also posts the recording link on the record's timeline.
- **Calls from inside Bitrix24**: click a phone number in Bitrix24's CRM and TalkChief places the call through your TalkChief phone. See [Call from Bitrix24](#call-from-bitrix24).
- **Adds the AI call summary, if you want**: with [AI transcription](/ai/ai-transcription) on, TalkChief can post the call's AI summary on the record's timeline once it's ready, with a link to the full transcript. This is off until you turn it on (see [Choose what gets logged](#choose-what-gets-logged)).

Each logged call shows:

| In Bitrix24 | What TalkChief puts there |
| --- | --- |
| Call type | Incoming or outgoing |
| Phone number | The other party's number |
| Start time and duration | When the call started, and its talk time |
| Call status | The call's result (see the table below) |
| User | The agent's Bitrix24 user, else the record's responsible user, else the user who connected TalkChief |
| CRM record | The matching contact, company or lead, or the new lead or contact |
| Recording | The call recording, when there is one |
| Timeline comment | The direction, time, result and talk time, the recording link and the agent, ending with **Logged by TalkChief**. Posted when the call has a recording or went to voicemail |

| TalkChief call result | Bitrix24 call status |
| --- | --- |
| Answered | Successful call |
| No answer, abandoned or voicemail | Missed call (the timeline comment says **Voicemail** for voicemail) |
| Busy | Busy |
| Cancelled by the caller | Call canceled |
| Network busy | Direction unavailable |
| Failed | Undefined |

Calls usually appear in Bitrix24 within a few minutes of hanging up.

## Before you start

- You are a **company admin** in TalkChief.
- Your Bitrix24 is on a **commercial plan** or in its trial. Bitrix24 offers its REST API, which TalkChief uses, only on commercial plans.
- You can install apps in your Bitrix24.
- You know your portal address: the address you see in your browser when you are signed in to Bitrix24, such as `yourcompany.bitrix24.com`, or your own domain for self-hosted Bitrix24.
- For self-hosted Bitrix24: the **Telephony** module is installed and turned on, your portal is reachable from the internet over HTTPS on the standard port (443), and it can reach Bitrix24's authorization server.
- One Bitrix24 portal can be connected to one TalkChief company.

## Connect Bitrix24

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations in TalkChief">
    In the TalkChief web app, select **Integrations** under **Configuration** in the sidebar. The page opens on the **CRM** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your portal address">
    On the **Bitrix24** card, select **Connect**. In the **Connect Bitrix24** window, enter your portal address in **Bitrix24 address** (for example `yourcompany.bitrix24.com`), then select **Connect**. TalkChief sends you to your Bitrix24 portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and approve access">
    Sign in to Bitrix24 if asked, and allow the TalkChief app the access it asks for (see [Data TalkChief uses in Bitrix24](#data-talkchief-uses-in-bitrix24)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm in TalkChief">
    Bitrix24 sends you back to **Integrations** in TalkChief. A message confirms that Bitrix24 is connected, and the Bitrix24 card moves to your connected integrations with a **Connected** label.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test with a real call">
    Make or receive a call with TalkChief. A few minutes after you hang up, open the contact or lead in Bitrix24 and check that the call is on its timeline.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose what gets logged

On the connected Bitrix24 card in **Integrations**, select the settings (gear) icon. The **Bitrix24 settings** window shows the connected portal and four switches you can change at any time. Each change applies to calls that end afterwards.

**Log calls** and **Log missed calls** are on by default. **Create leads for new numbers** and **Add the AI call summary to each call** are off by default for new connections. **Add the AI call summary to each call** needs [AI transcription](/ai/ai-transcription): without it, the switch is locked and shows **Needs AI transcription.** with a **Turn it on** link to the AI transcription settings, or **Needs a plan with AI transcription.** with a **See plans** link when your plan doesn't include it.

| Setting | On | Off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Log calls** | Every external call is logged | Nothing is logged, and the connection stays in place |
| **Log missed calls** | Busy, unanswered, cancelled and voicemail calls are logged too | Only answered calls are logged |
| **Create leads for new numbers** | Unknown numbers become a new lead (or a new contact when your CRM works without leads) | Calls from unknown numbers are logged without a CRM record |
| **Add the AI call summary to each call** | When a call's AI summary is ready, it appears as a timeline comment on the call's contact, company or lead, with a link to the full transcript | No summary is posted |

The summary is posted only for calls that AI transcription covers and that are logged on a CRM record; a call logged without a record gets no summary. It is a timeline comment of its own, separate from the call, and looks like this:

```text
[AI summary]
The customer asked to move their delivery to Friday. The agent confirmed the new date and will send a confirmation email.

Full transcript in TalkChief: https://...
[End of AI summary]
```

If a summary is created again for the same call, TalkChief updates its earlier summary comment instead of adding a second one. Anything you typed into that comment outside the summary stays as it is. If you delete the summary comment in Bitrix24, a later summary of the call is posted as a new comment.

## Call from Bitrix24

Agents can call a customer by clicking a phone number in Bitrix24's CRM. Bitrix24 opens its own call card, and you talk on your usual TalkChief phone (the TalkChief app or your desk phone), so moving between Bitrix24 pages never drops a call.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a TalkChief number for outgoing calls">
    When you connect, TalkChief adds your company's TalkChief numbers to Bitrix24's telephony as lines named **TalkChief (+number)**, or **TalkChief** followed by the number's name in TalkChief, such as **TalkChief Sales (+number)**. In Bitrix24's telephony settings, a Bitrix24 administrator picks one of them as the **Default Number for Outgoing Calls**. Each user can also choose TalkChief as their default in their own Bitrix24 user settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click a phone number">
    Click the phone number on a contact, company or lead. Bitrix24 opens its call card, and TalkChief rings your TalkChief phone first. Answer it, and TalkChief dials the number. Hang up on your phone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the call afterwards">
    When the call ends, TalkChief logs it on that same Bitrix24 call, with its result, talk time and recording. Each call appears once in Bitrix24's call statistics and on the record.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  TalkChief finds you by email: your Bitrix24 user's email must be the same as your email in the TalkChief company connected to this portal. Your TalkChief phone must be online to place calls: keep the TalkChief app open or your desk phone connected. TalkChief checks for new TalkChief numbers once a day and adds them as lines. Numbers you remove in TalkChief stay in Bitrix24's list.
</Note>

Bitrix24's CRM callback form (a visitor asks on your website to be called back) is not supported yet: TalkChief receives the request but places no call.

## Data TalkChief uses in Bitrix24

The TalkChief app asks Bitrix24 for these permissions:

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Telephony | Save each call in Bitrix24's telephony and attach its recording, add your TalkChief numbers as lines, and place the calls you start by clicking a number |
| CRM | Find the record that matches the number, check whether your CRM uses leads, create a lead or contact for a new number (only while **Create leads for new numbers** is on), post the recording link on the record's timeline, and post the AI call summary there (only while **Add the AI call summary to each call** is on) |
| Users (basic) | Match the TalkChief agent to their Bitrix24 user by email, and identify the user who connected TalkChief |

TalkChief writes calls, timeline comments and new leads or contacts to Bitrix24. It does not change or delete your existing deals, contacts, companies or other records, and it does not copy your CRM data into TalkChief. Bitrix24 also tells TalkChief when the app is installed or uninstalled, and when someone clicks a phone number to call through TalkChief.

## Disconnect Bitrix24

To stop logging calls, open **Integrations** in TalkChief and select the trash icon on the Bitrix24 card (or **Disconnect** in the **Bitrix24 settings** window), then confirm with **Yes, disconnect**.

When you disconnect:

- Calls are no longer logged in Bitrix24. Calls that were waiting to be logged are dropped.
- Clicking a phone number in Bitrix24 no longer places a TalkChief call. The TalkChief lines stay in Bitrix24's telephony settings, so choose another **Default Number for Outgoing Calls** there if your team keeps calling from Bitrix24.
- TalkChief deletes its access to your portal.
- Calls, comments, leads and contacts already in Bitrix24 stay there. Nothing is deleted from Bitrix24.
- The TalkChief app stays installed in Bitrix24 until you uninstall it there.

You can connect again at any time from the Bitrix24 card.

## Uninstall the app from Bitrix24

You can also remove TalkChief from inside Bitrix24 by uninstalling the TalkChief app. Bitrix24 then removes the app's access and tells TalkChief, which disconnects the Bitrix24 card. If the card still shows **Connected** or later shows **Reconnect needed**, disconnect it on the card.

Calls, comments, leads and contacts already in Bitrix24 stay there.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="TalkChief says my Bitrix24 address is not valid">
    Enter the address you see in your browser when you are signed in to Bitrix24, such as `yourcompany.bitrix24.com` or `crm.yourcompany.com`. IP addresses, addresses with a port number and local network names are not accepted.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Connecting ends with Could not connect Bitrix24">
    Check that your Bitrix24 is on a commercial plan or in its trial: Bitrix24 refuses its REST API on other plans, and TalkChief stops the connection when that happens. For self-hosted Bitrix24, also check that your portal is reachable from the internet over HTTPS on port 443 and can reach Bitrix24's authorization server. Then select **Connect** again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The Bitrix24 card asks me to reconnect">
    The card shows **Reconnect needed** when Bitrix24 no longer accepts TalkChief's access, for example after the app was uninstalled, the plan changed to one without REST, or the Telephony module was turned off on self-hosted Bitrix24. Fix the cause, then select **Reconnect** and approve access again. Calls that ended in the meantime are logged once you reconnect, as long as you reconnect within 7 days.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="This Bitrix24 portal is already connected to another company">
    A Bitrix24 portal can be connected to only one TalkChief company, so that each company's calls stay in its own CRM. Disconnect it from the other TalkChief company first, or connect a different portal.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A call is logged under the wrong user">
    TalkChief logs the call under the active Bitrix24 user whose email matches the agent's TalkChief email. When none matches, the call goes to the responsible user of the record that has the number, or to the user who connected TalkChief when no record has it. Use the same email in both, or add the agent as a user in Bitrix24.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A call is not on the contact or lead">
    Bitrix24 matches the record by its phone number. Check that the record has the caller's number. If **Create leads for new numbers** is off, calls from numbers your CRM doesn't have are logged without a record.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Clicking a phone number doesn't call through TalkChief">
    Check that a TalkChief line is chosen as the **Default Number for Outgoing Calls** in Bitrix24's telephony settings, or as your default in your Bitrix24 user settings. If no TalkChief line is listed, TalkChief hasn't finished setting up your portal yet: it finishes when it logs the next call. If the lines are still missing after that, contact TalkChief support.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The call card closes right after I click a number">
    TalkChief couldn't place the call and ended it in Bitrix24 with one of these reasons:

    | Reason | What to do |
    | --- | --- |
    | No TalkChief user for this Bitrix24 user | Use the same email in Bitrix24 and TalkChief, in the TalkChief company connected to this portal |
    | TalkChief user not set up for calls | Your TalkChief user isn't fully set up for calling yet. Contact TalkChief support |
    | TalkChief phone offline | Open the TalkChief app on your computer or phone, or check that your desk phone is connected, then try again |
    | Already on a TalkChief call | Finish your current call first |
    | Invalid number | Check the number on the record, preferably with its country code |
    | TalkChief telephony unavailable or TalkChief could not place the call | Try again in a moment. If it keeps happening, contact TalkChief support |
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A call has no AI summary">
    Check that **Add the AI call summary to each call** is on in the **Bitrix24 settings** window. If the switch is locked, your company needs [AI transcription](/ai/ai-transcription) first. Only calls that AI transcription covers get a summary; short calls, internal calls and calls from before it was turned on are not transcribed. The summary is posted after the call has been transcribed, so it can appear a while after the call itself. A call logged without a contact, company or lead gets no summary, because there is no record to post it on.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A recording is missing">
    The recording is added only when the call was recorded: check that call recording is on for that number or team in TalkChief. Bitrix24 downloads the recording from TalkChief after the call; the timeline comment also has the recording link.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

For anything else, contact TalkChief support at [support@talkchief.io](mailto:support@talkchief.io).
