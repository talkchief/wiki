TalkChief for Zendesk turns every TalkChief phone call into a Zendesk Support ticket for the caller, without anyone typing it in. You connect once with your Zendesk subdomain and your Zendesk login, with no API tokens to copy, and from then on each finished call appears as a ticket with the numbers, talk time, the agent who answered and the call recording.

## What the integration does

- **Logs every external call as a ticket**: each inbound, outbound or voicemail call becomes one ticket. Calls between two TalkChief extensions are not logged.
- **Shows the call like a phone ticket**: on Zendesk plans that include Talk Partner Edition (all Zendesk Suite plans), the ticket is a phone-call ticket. Its call details show the numbers, start time, talk time, the agent who answered, and a player for the recording. On other plans, the ticket gets an internal note with the same details and a link to the recording.
- **Finds the caller**: TalkChief looks up the caller's number (or the number you dialed) among your Zendesk users and makes that user the ticket's requester.
- **Creates users for new numbers, if you want**: when **Create leads for new numbers** is on, a number no Zendesk user has gets a new end user with the caller ID name and the number. It is off by default: the call is then logged with the agent as requester and the tag `talkchief_unknown_caller`.
- **Assigns the ticket to the agent**: answered calls are assigned to the Zendesk agent whose email matches the TalkChief agent's email, or to the Zendesk admin who connected TalkChief when there is no match. Missed calls stay unassigned for your Zendesk routing.
- **Calls from inside Zendesk**: with the TalkChief app in Zendesk's top bar, click a phone number and TalkChief places the call through your TalkChief phone. Incoming calls open the caller's profile, and after a call you can add a note and an outcome. See [Call from Zendesk](#call-from-zendesk).
- **Adds the AI call summary, if you want**: with [AI transcription](/ai/ai-transcription) on, TalkChief can add the call's AI summary to the call's ticket as an internal note once it's ready, with a link to the full transcript. This is off until you turn it on (see [Choose what gets logged](#choose-what-gets-logged)).

Each ticket shows:

| Zendesk field | What TalkChief puts there |
| --- | --- |
| Subject | **Inbound call from**, **Outbound call to** or **Voicemail from**, followed by the other party's number |
| Channel | Phone call (inbound or outbound) or voicemail, on plans with Talk Partner Edition |
| Requester | The Zendesk user with the caller's number |
| Assignee | On answered calls, the agent's Zendesk user when their emails match, else the Zendesk admin who connected TalkChief |
| Call details | From and to numbers, start time, talk time, the agent who answered, and the recording |
| Tags | `talkchief`; the call's result `talkchief_call_answered`, `talkchief_call_no_answer`, `talkchief_call_busy`, `talkchief_call_voicemail` or `talkchief_call_failed`; `talkchief_unknown_caller` when the ticket has no caller record; and the outcome an agent picks after the call (see [Add a note after the call](#add-a-note-after-the-call)) |

Calls usually appear in Zendesk within a few minutes of hanging up.

## Before you start

- You are a **company admin** in TalkChief.
- You sign in to Zendesk as an **admin** of the Zendesk account you want to connect. Agent and end-user logins can't connect.
- You know your Zendesk subdomain: the first part of the address you use to sign in, such as `yourcompany` in `yourcompany.zendesk.com`. Custom domains (host-mapped addresses) can't be used to connect; use the `zendesk.com` address.
- One Zendesk account can be connected to one TalkChief company.

## Connect Zendesk

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations in TalkChief">
    In the TalkChief web app, select **Integrations** under **Configuration** in the sidebar. The page opens on the **CRM** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your Zendesk address">
    On the **Zendesk** card, select **Connect**. In the **Connect Zendesk** window, enter your subdomain or full address in **Zendesk address** (for example `yourcompany.zendesk.com`), then select **Connect**. TalkChief sends you to your Zendesk account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and approve access">
    Sign in to Zendesk if asked. Zendesk shows the access TalkChief asks for (see [Data TalkChief uses in Zendesk](#data-talkchief-uses-in-zendesk)). Approve it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm in TalkChief">
    Zendesk sends you back to **Integrations** in TalkChief. A message confirms that Zendesk is connected, and the Zendesk card moves to your connected integrations with a **Connected** label.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test with a real call">
    Make or receive a call with TalkChief. A few minutes after you hang up, open Zendesk and check that a ticket for the call is in your views.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose what gets logged

On the connected Zendesk card in **Integrations**, select the settings (gear) icon. The settings window shows the connected Zendesk account and the switches you can change at any time. Each change applies to calls that end afterwards.

| Setting | Default | On | Off |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Log calls** | On | Every external call is logged | Nothing is logged, and the connection stays in place |
| **Log missed calls** | On | Busy, unanswered, cancelled and voicemail calls are logged too | Only answered calls are logged |
| **Create leads for new numbers** | Off | Unknown numbers get a new Zendesk end user, who becomes the requester | No user is created; the ticket's requester is the agent who answered (or the Zendesk admin who connected TalkChief), and the ticket is tagged `talkchief_unknown_caller` |
| **Add the AI call summary to each call** | Off | When a call's AI summary is ready, it's added to the call's ticket as an internal note, with a link to the full transcript | Tickets get no summary |

### AI call summary

**Add the AI call summary to each call** needs [AI transcription](/ai/ai-transcription). Without it, the switch is locked and shows **Needs AI transcription.** with a **Turn it on** link to **Settings** > **AI transcription**. If your plan doesn't include AI transcription, it shows **Needs a plan with AI transcription.** with a **See plans** link instead.

When the switch is on, TalkChief adds the summary to the call's ticket as an internal note, so only your agents see it. The note looks like this:

```text
[AI summary]
The customer asked to move their delivery to Friday. The agent confirmed the new date and will send a confirmation email.

Full transcript in TalkChief: https://...
[End of AI summary]
```

The summary is added only to calls that AI transcription covers, after the call has been transcribed, so it can appear a while after the ticket itself. Zendesk can't edit comments, so if a call's summary is created again with different text, TalkChief adds a new internal note starting with **[AI summary, updated]**. The newest summary note is the current one. An unchanged summary adds nothing, and earlier notes are never changed or removed.

## Call from Zendesk

The TalkChief app adds a TalkChief panel to the top bar of Zendesk Support, so each agent can place and receive TalkChief calls while working in Zendesk. You talk on your usual TalkChief phone (the TalkChief app or your desk phone), so moving between tickets never drops a call.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the TalkChief app in Zendesk">
    A Zendesk admin installs the **TalkChief** app in your Zendesk account. Its icon then appears in the top bar of Zendesk Support. Your TalkChief admin must also have connected Zendesk (see [Connect Zendesk](#connect-zendesk)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to TalkChief">
    Open the TalkChief panel from the top bar and select **Sign in**. A TalkChief window opens; sign in if asked, and it closes by itself when you are connected. Each agent signs in once on their own computer. Signing in here doesn't sign you out of the TalkChief web app or your TalkChief apps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place a call">
    Click a phone number in a ticket or a user profile, or type a number in the panel and select **Call**. TalkChief rings your TalkChief phone first. Answer it, and TalkChief dials the number. Hang up on your phone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive a call">
    When a call rings your TalkChief phone, the TalkChief panel opens and Zendesk shows the caller's profile. Answer on your TalkChief phone as usual. Zendesk loads the panel the first time you open it, so open the TalkChief panel once after you load Zendesk to get these pop-ups.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A call you place from a ticket is added to that ticket when it ends. Other calls get their own ticket, like every logged call.

<Note>
  Clicking phone numbers in Zendesk needs Talk Partner Edition, which all Zendesk Suite plans include. Without it, type the number in the TalkChief panel instead. Your TalkChief phone must be online to place calls: keep the TalkChief app open or your desk phone connected.
</Note>

### Add a note after the call

When a call ends, the panel lets you type a note and pick an outcome (connected, left voicemail, no answer, busy, wrong number). TalkChief adds them to the call's ticket:

- The note becomes an internal note on the ticket, starting with **[Agent note]**.
- The outcome becomes a tag such as `talkchief_outcome_busy`.

You can change them later from the panel. Zendesk can't edit comments, so a changed note is added as a new internal note starting with **[Agent note, updated]**, and a removed note adds **[Agent note removed]**. The newest TalkChief note is the current one. A changed outcome replaces the old tag.

## Data TalkChief uses in Zendesk

When you connect, you allow TalkChief to read and write data in your Zendesk account. TalkChief uses that access to:

| Access | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Read users | Find the user whose phone number matches the call, and the agent whose email matches the TalkChief agent |
| Create users | Create an end user for a number Zendesk doesn't have (only while **Create leads for new numbers** is on) |
| Create and update tickets | Create one ticket per call, with its call details or internal note, and add the AI call summary as an internal note (only while **Add the AI call summary to each call** is on) |

TalkChief does not read, change or delete your other tickets, users, organizations or settings, and it does not copy your Zendesk data into TalkChief.

## Disconnect Zendesk

To stop logging calls and remove TalkChief's access, open **Integrations** in TalkChief and select the trash icon on the Zendesk card, then confirm.

When you disconnect:

- Calls are no longer logged in Zendesk. Calls that were waiting to be logged are dropped.
- TalkChief deletes its access token for your Zendesk account.
- Tickets and users already in Zendesk stay there. Nothing is deleted from Zendesk.

You can connect again at any time from the Zendesk card.

## Remove TalkChief's access from Zendesk

A Zendesk admin can also end TalkChief's access from inside Zendesk by deleting the OAuth tokens of the TalkChief client in Admin Center. Calls then stop being logged and the Zendesk card in TalkChief shows **Reconnect needed**. Tickets and users already in Zendesk stay there. To remove the connection from TalkChief too, disconnect it on the Zendesk card.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="TalkChief says my Zendesk address is not valid">
    Enter your Zendesk subdomain (`yourcompany`) or the address ending in `.zendesk.com`. Custom domains such as `support.yourcompany.com` are not accepted.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The Zendesk card asks me to reconnect">
    The card shows **Reconnect needed** when TalkChief lost access to Zendesk, usually because its access was removed in Zendesk or the admin who connected it lost their role. Select **Reconnect** and approve access again. Calls that ended in the meantime are logged once you reconnect, as long as you reconnect within 7 days.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="This Zendesk account is already connected to another company">
    A Zendesk account can be connected to only one TalkChief company, so that each company's calls stay in its own account. Disconnect it from the other TalkChief company first, or connect a different Zendesk account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Tickets have an internal note instead of call details">
    Phone-call tickets need Talk Partner Edition, which all Zendesk Suite plans include. Other plans need Talk Partner Edition licences for your agents. Without it, TalkChief logs each call as a ticket with an internal note. TalkChief tries phone-call tickets again every week, and every time you reconnect.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A ticket has no assignee">
    TalkChief assigns answered calls only when the agent's TalkChief email matches a Zendesk agent's email, and only if Zendesk accepts that agent as assignee. Use the same email in both, or add the agent to Zendesk.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A ticket has the wrong requester">
    TalkChief matches the caller by the phone number on the Zendesk user. Check that the user's phone number is saved with its country code (for example `+15551234567`). If **Create leads for new numbers** is off, calls from unknown numbers use the answering agent as requester.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A ticket has no AI summary">
    Check that **Add the AI call summary to each call** is on in the Zendesk settings window; if it is locked, turn on [AI transcription](/ai/ai-transcription) first. Only calls that AI transcription covers get a summary: short calls, internal calls and calls from before it was turned on are not transcribed. The summary is added after the call has been transcribed, so it can appear a while after the call. It's not added to a ticket that is closed, or that was deleted.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Clicking a phone number opens Zendesk Talk instead of TalkChief">
    Zendesk sends **Call this number** to its own Zendesk Talk while you are available in Zendesk Talk, and to TalkChief only while you are offline there. No app can change this, so the TalkChief panel shows a notice while Zendesk Talk takes your clicks. Ask your Zendesk admin to turn Zendesk Talk off for the agents who call with TalkChief, or set yourself offline in Zendesk Talk. Until then, type or paste the number in the TalkChief panel.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Clicking a phone number in Zendesk does nothing">
    Click-to-call reaches the TalkChief app only on Zendesk accounts with Talk Partner Edition, and only after you signed in to the TalkChief panel. Without Talk Partner Edition, the panel shows a message and you can type the number in the panel.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The panel says it is signed in to a different Zendesk account">
    The TalkChief panel only works in the Zendesk account your TalkChief company connected. If you signed in to the panel with a TalkChief user of another company, select **Sign out** and sign in with your user in the matching TalkChief company.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The TalkChief panel asks me to sign in again">
    Select **Sign in** to continue. If nothing opens, allow pop-ups for Zendesk in your browser.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A recording does not play">
    Zendesk keeps only a link to the recording and plays it in the agent's browser. The recording is added only when the call was recorded: check that call recording is on for that number or team in TalkChief.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

For anything else, contact TalkChief support at [support@talkchief.io](mailto:support@talkchief.io).
