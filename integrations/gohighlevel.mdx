The TalkChief app for HighLevel (GoHighLevel) logs every TalkChief phone call in your HighLevel sub-account without anyone typing it in. You connect once by installing the app in your sub-account, with no API keys to copy, and from then on each finished call appears in the matching contact's conversation with its result and recording, plus a note with the talk time and the agent who handled it.

## What the app does

- **Logs every external call**: inbound and outbound calls are logged as call entries in the contact's conversation. Calls between two TalkChief extensions are not logged.
- **Finds the right contact**: TalkChief looks up the caller's number (or the number you dialed) among the contacts of the connected sub-account.
- **Creates contacts for new numbers, if you want**: with **Create leads for new numbers** on, a number no contact has gets a new contact with the number and the caller ID name, assigned to the agent. It is off for new connections, and calls from unknown numbers are then not logged (see below).
- **Adds the recording**: when the call was recorded, the call entry carries a link to the recording, and the note repeats it.
- **Credits the agent**: the note is written by the HighLevel user whose email matches the TalkChief agent's email.
- **Adds the AI call summary, if you want**: with [AI transcription](/ai/ai-transcription) on, TalkChief can add the call's AI summary to the same note once it's ready, with a link to the full transcript. This is off until you turn it on (see [Choose what gets logged](#choose-what-gets-logged)).
- **Calls from HighLevel**: the TalkChief calling panel places calls through your TalkChief phone and opens the caller's contact when your phone rings. With TalkChief's click-to-dial script, a TalkChief call button next to each phone number dials it in one click. See [Call from HighLevel](#call-from-highlevel).

Each logged call adds:

| In HighLevel | What TalkChief puts there |
| --- | --- |
| Call entry in the contact's conversation | Direction (inbound or outbound), the numbers on the call, the call status and the recording link when there is one. On incoming calls, the entry's text also shows the talk time and the agent |
| Call status | Answered calls show as completed; busy, unanswered, cancelled, voicemail and failed calls show as such |
| Note on the contact | **Inbound call from** or **Outbound call to** with the other party's number, the status, talk time, the agent's name and extension, and the recording link |
| New contact (only for unknown numbers) | The number, the caller ID name when there is one, source **TalkChief**, assigned to the agent |

With **Add the AI call summary to each call** on, the summary is added at the end of the same note after the call has been transcribed:

```text
[AI summary]
The customer asked to move their delivery to Friday. The agent confirmed the new date and will send a confirmation email.

Full transcript in TalkChief: https://...
[End of AI summary]
```

Calls usually appear in HighLevel within a few minutes of hanging up.

## Before you start

- You are a **company admin** in TalkChief.
- In HighLevel, you can install apps in the sub-account you want to connect.
- One HighLevel sub-account can be connected to one TalkChief company. You can install the app from your agency (HighLevel's **Agency View**, which its install page opens in) or from the sub-account itself. If an agency install covers several sub-accounts, TalkChief asks which one to connect.

## Connect HighLevel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations in TalkChief">
    In the TalkChief web app, select **Integrations** under **Configuration** in the sidebar. The page opens on the **CRM** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the connection">
    On the **HighLevel** card, select **Connect**. TalkChief sends you to HighLevel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the sub-account and approve access">
    Sign in to HighLevel if asked. Choose the sub-account whose calls you want to log, review the access TalkChief asks for (see [Data TalkChief uses in HighLevel](#data-talkchief-uses-in-highlevel)) and approve it. Installing from **Agency View** works too: choose the sub-account (or sub-accounts) HighLevel installs the app in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm in TalkChief">
    HighLevel sends you back to **Integrations** in TalkChief. A message confirms that HighLevel is connected, and the HighLevel card moves to your connected integrations with a **Connected** label. If you installed the app from the agency into more than one sub-account, TalkChief first asks you to **Choose your HighLevel business account**: pick the sub-account whose calls you want to log and select **Use this business account**. TalkChief logs calls only into that one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test with a real call">
    Make or receive a call with TalkChief. A few minutes after you hang up, open the contact in HighLevel and check its conversation for the call and its notes for the TalkChief note.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose what gets logged

On the connected HighLevel card in **Integrations**, select the settings (gear) icon. The **HighLevel settings** window shows the connected sub-account and switches you can change at any time. Each change applies to calls that end afterwards. **Add the AI call summary to each call** appears only when your company has [AI transcription](/ai/ai-transcription).

| Setting | New connections | On | Off |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Log calls** | On | Every external call is logged | Nothing is logged, and the connection stays in place |
| **Log missed calls** | On | Busy, unanswered, cancelled and voicemail calls are logged too | Only answered calls are logged |
| **Create leads for new numbers** | Off | Unknown numbers get a new HighLevel contact | Calls from numbers HighLevel doesn't have are not logged |
| **Add the AI call summary to each call** | Off | When a call's AI summary is ready, it's added to the call's note with a link to the full transcript | Notes have no summary |

The summary is added only to calls that AI transcription covers. If a summary is created again, TalkChief replaces its earlier summary in the note instead of adding a second one. Anything else in the note stays as it is.

<Note>
  HighLevel logs calls only on a contact, so with **Create leads for new numbers** off, a call from or to a number that no contact has is skipped.
</Note>

## Call from HighLevel

The TalkChief calling panel lets each agent place TalkChief calls and see incoming ones while working in HighLevel. You talk on your usual TalkChief phone (the TalkChief app or your desk phone), and the panel runs in its own small window, so moving between HighLevel pages never closes it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the panel">
    In your HighLevel sub-account, select **TalkChief** in the left menu, then **Open the TalkChief panel**. The panel opens in its own window; keep it open while you work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to TalkChief">
    In the panel, select **Sign in**. A TalkChief window opens; sign in if asked, and it closes by itself when you are connected. Each agent signs in once per browser. Signing in here doesn't sign you out of the TalkChief web app or your TalkChief apps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow pop-ups for TalkChief">
    When your browser asks, allow pop-ups for app.talkchief.io. The panel needs them to open the caller's contact in HighLevel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place a call">
    Type or paste the number on the panel's dial pad and select **Call**. TalkChief rings your TalkChief phone first. Answer it, and TalkChief dials the number. Hang up on your phone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive a call">
    When a call rings your TalkChief phone, the panel shows it and opens the caller's contact in a HighLevel tab. If your browser blocked that, select **Open record** in the panel. Answer on your TalkChief phone as usual.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Calls made or received with the panel are logged once, like every other call: a call entry in the contact's conversation and a note with the talk time and agent.

### Click a number to call

With TalkChief's click-to-dial script, a small blue TalkChief call button appears next to phone numbers in HighLevel:

- in the **Contacts** list, right after HighLevel's own call icon,
- next to the phone field on a contact,
- in the contact's conversation header, next to HighLevel's call button.

Select it, and the TalkChief panel calls that number. HighLevel's own call buttons stay where they are and keep using HighLevel's phone system.

An **agency admin** adds the script once, and it then works in every sub-account of the agency.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the script">
    Open **The click-to-dial script** below and copy all of it with the copy button. Don't change it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your agency's Custom JS">
    In HighLevel, switch to **Agency View** and go to **Settings**, then **Company**, then the **White Label** tab. Scroll to **Custom JS**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste and save">
    Paste the script into the **Custom JS** editor, after anything already there. Keep any other scripts you have. Select **Save changes** under the editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it">
    Reload HighLevel and open **Contacts** in a sub-account that is connected to TalkChief. A blue TalkChief call button appears next to each phone number. In **Agency View** there are no buttons.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Accordion title="The click-to-dial script">

```html
<script>
/*
 * TalkChief click-to-dial for HighLevel (Custom JS).
 *
 * Adds a small TalkChief call button next to phone numbers in a business
 * account (contact list, contact details, conversation header). HighLevel's
 * own call buttons stay as they are. A click hands the number (and the
 * contact, when known) to the TalkChief panel window:
 * - a panel is open: postMessage to it, for https://app.talkchief.io only;
 * - none is open: open the panel with the number (?dial=...).
 * The panel dials only for the business account it is connected to.
 *
 * Static and self-contained: no token, no network request, no data stored.
 * Paste it between <script> and </script> in HighLevel's Custom JS.
 * Source: talkchief_frontend, integrations/gohighlevel/click-to-dial.js.
 */
(function () {
  "use strict";
  if (window.__talkchiefClickToDial) return;
  window.__talkchiefClickToDial = true;

  var PANEL_ORIGIN = "https://app.talkchief.io";
  var PANEL_PATH = "/crm-dialer/gohighlevel";
  var PANEL_WINDOW = "talkchief-ghl-panel";
  var SOURCE = "talkchief-ghl";
  var MARK = "data-talkchief-dial";
  var ID = /^[A-Za-z0-9]{8,64}$/;
  var NANP = ["US", "CA", "PR"];

  var locationId = null;
  var country = "";

  /* The business account the agent is in (none in the agency view). */
  function readLocation() {
    var u = window.AppUtils && window.AppUtils.Utilities;
    if (!u || typeof u.getCurrentLocation !== "function") return Promise.resolve();
    return Promise.resolve(u.getCurrentLocation()).then(
      function (loc) {
        locationId = loc && typeof loc.id === "string" && ID.test(loc.id) ? loc.id : null;
        country = loc && loc.address && loc.address.country ? String(loc.address.country).toUpperCase() : "";
      },
      function () {
        locationId = null;
      }
    );
  }

  /* E.164 where the number says so (or a 10-digit US/Canada number); else digits for the panel to resolve. */
  function toDial(raw) {
    var s = String(raw || "").trim();
    var digits = s.replace(/\D/g, "");
    if (digits.length < 7 || digits.length > 15) return null;
    if (s.charAt(0) === "+") return "+" + digits;
    if (s.indexOf("00") === 0) return "+" + digits.slice(2);
    if (NANP.indexOf(country) >= 0) {
      if (digits.length === 10) return "+1" + digits;
      if (digits.length === 11 && digits.charAt(0) === "1") return "+" + digits;
    }
    return digits;
  }

  function contactFromHref(href) {
    var m = /\/contacts\/detail\/([A-Za-z0-9]{8,64})(?:[/?#]|$)/.exec(href || "");
    return m ? m[1] : null;
  }

  function notice(text) {
    var el = document.createElement("div");
    el.setAttribute("role", "status");
    el.textContent = text;
    el.style.cssText =
      "position:fixed;right:16px;bottom:16px;z-index:2147483647;max-width:320px;padding:10px 14px;" +
      "border-radius:8px;background:#10293b;color:#fff;font:13px/1.4 sans-serif;box-shadow:0 4px 16px rgba(0,0,0,.2)";
    document.body.appendChild(el);
    setTimeout(function () {
      el.remove();
    }, 6000);
  }

  function panelUrl(number, contactId) {
    var q = "location_id=" + encodeURIComponent(locationId) + "&crm_origin=" + encodeURIComponent(window.location.origin);
    q += "&dial=" + encodeURIComponent(number);
    if (contactId) q += "&contact_id=" + encodeURIComponent(contactId);
    return PANEL_ORIGIN + PANEL_PATH + "?" + q;
  }

  function dial(raw, contactId) {
    var number = toDial(raw);
    if (!number || !locationId) return;
    var w = window.open("", PANEL_WINDOW, "width=400,height=680");
    if (!w) {
      notice("Allow pop-ups for this site to call with TalkChief.");
      return;
    }
    var fresh = false;
    try {
      fresh = w.location.href === "about:blank";
    } catch (e) {
      fresh = false; /* cross-origin: the TalkChief panel is already open */
    }
    if (fresh) {
      w.location.replace(panelUrl(number, contactId));
    } else {
      var msg = { source: SOURCE, type: "dial", number: number, locationId: locationId };
      if (contactId) msg.contactId = contactId;
      w.postMessage(msg, PANEL_ORIGIN);
    }
    try {
      w.focus();
    } catch (e) {
      /* the browser decides */
    }
  }

  window.addEventListener("message", function (e) {
    var d = e.data;
    if (e.origin !== PANEL_ORIGIN || !d || d.source !== "talkchief-panel" || d.type !== "dial-result") return;
    if (d.ok === false && d.reason === "other-account") {
      notice("The TalkChief panel is open for another business account. Open it again from this business account's TalkChief menu.");
    }
  });

  var SVG = "http://www.w3.org/2000/svg";
  var PHONE =
    "M22 16.9v3a2 2 0 0 1-2.2 2 19.8 19.8 0 0 1-8.6-3.1 19.5 19.5 0 0 1-6-6A19.8 19.8 0 0 1 2.1 4.2 2 2 0 0 1 " +
    "4.1 2h3a2 2 0 0 1 2 1.7c.1 1 .4 1.9.7 2.8a2 2 0 0 1-.5 2.1L8 9.9a16 16 0 0 0 6 6l1.3-1.3a2 2 0 0 1 2.1-.4c.9.3 " +
    "1.8.6 2.8.7a2 2 0 0 1 1.7 2z";

  function icon() {
    var svg = document.createElementNS(SVG, "svg");
    var attrs = { viewBox: "0 0 24 24", width: "14", height: "14", fill: "none", stroke: "currentColor", "stroke-width": "2", "aria-hidden": "true" };
    Object.keys(attrs).forEach(function (k) {
      svg.setAttribute(k, attrs[k]);
    });
    var path = document.createElementNS(SVG, "path");
    path.setAttribute("d", PHONE);
    path.setAttribute("stroke-linejoin", "round");
    svg.appendChild(path);
    return svg;
  }

  /* One TalkChief button; read(): {number, contactId} at click time (HighLevel re-renders its rows). */
  function button(read) {
    var b = document.createElement("button");
    b.type = "button";
    b.setAttribute(MARK, "");
    b.title = "Call with TalkChief";
    b.setAttribute("aria-label", "Call with TalkChief");
    b.appendChild(icon());
    b.style.cssText =
      "display:inline-flex;align-items:center;justify-content:center;flex:none;width:22px;height:22px;" +
      "margin:0 4px;padding:0;border:0;border-radius:50%;background:#0f93f3;color:#fff;cursor:pointer;vertical-align:middle";
    b.addEventListener("click", function (e) {
      e.preventDefault();
      e.stopPropagation();
      var t = read();
      if (t) dial(t.number, t.contactId);
    });
    return b;
  }

  /* The first text in el that holds a number (HighLevel's cell shows it twice, e.g. formatted and as a tooltip). */
  function firstNumber(el) {
    var walk = document.createTreeWalker(el, NodeFilter.SHOW_TEXT);
    for (var n = walk.nextNode(); n; n = walk.nextNode()) {
      if (n.nodeValue.replace(/\D/g, "").length >= 7) return n.nodeValue;
    }
    return "";
  }

  function pageContact() {
    return contactFromHref(window.location.pathname);
  }

  /* Where HighLevel shows numbers (checked 2026-11-11; HighLevel's markup, so re-check after their UI changes). */
  var SPOTS = [
    {
      /* Contact list: the phone cell, after HighLevel's own call icon. */
      find: '.tabulator-cell[tabulator-field="phone"]',
      add: function (cell, b) {
        var own = cell.querySelector(".phone-call-icon");
        if (own) own.insertAdjacentElement("afterend", b);
        else cell.insertBefore(b, cell.firstChild);
      },
      read: function (cell) {
        var row = cell.closest(".tabulator-row");
        var link = row && row.querySelector('a[href*="/contacts/detail/"]');
        return { number: firstNumber(cell), contactId: link ? contactFromHref(link.getAttribute("href")) : null };
      },
    },
    {
      /* Contact details: the phone field. */
      find: 'input.hr-input-phone[type="tel"]',
      host: function (input) {
        var box = input.closest(".hr-input-container");
        return box && box.parentElement && box.parentElement.parentElement;
      },
      add: function (row, b) {
        row.appendChild(b);
      },
      read: function (row) {
        var input = row.querySelector('input.hr-input-phone[type="tel"]');
        return { number: input ? input.value : "", contactId: pageContact() };
      },
    },
    {
      /* Contact and conversation header: next to HighLevel's call button. */
      find: '.header-calling-slot',
      add: function (slot, b) {
        slot.appendChild(b);
      },
      read: function (slot) {
        var own = slot.querySelector('[aria-label^="Call:"]');
        return { number: own ? own.getAttribute("aria-label").slice(5) : "", contactId: pageContact() };
      },
    },
    {
      /* Any tel: link. */
      find: 'a[href^="tel:"]',
      host: function (a) {
        return a.parentElement;
      },
      add: function (parent, b, a) {
        a.insertAdjacentElement("afterend", b);
      },
      read: function (parent) {
        var a = parent.querySelector('a[href^="tel:"]');
        return { number: a ? decodeURIComponent(a.getAttribute("href").slice(4)) : "", contactId: pageContact() };
      },
    },
  ];

  function removeAll() {
    var all = document.querySelectorAll("[" + MARK + "]");
    for (var i = 0; i < all.length; i++) all[i].remove();
  }

  /* One button for one number: its own function, so each click reads its own host. */
  function attach(spot, el) {
    var host = spot.host ? spot.host(el) : el;
    if (!host || host.querySelector("[" + MARK + "]")) return;
    var t = spot.read(host);
    if (!t || !toDial(t.number)) return;
    spot.add(
      host,
      button(function () {
        return spot.read(host);
      }),
      el
    );
  }

  function scan() {
    if (!locationId) {
      removeAll();
      return;
    }
    SPOTS.forEach(function (spot) {
      var found = document.querySelectorAll(spot.find);
      for (var i = 0; i < found.length; i++) attach(spot, found[i]);
    });
  }

  var timer = null;
  function soon() {
    if (timer) return;
    timer = setTimeout(function () {
      timer = null;
      scan();
    }, 300);
  }

  function refresh() {
    readLocation().then(soon, soon);
  }

  function ours(node) {
    return node.nodeType === 1 && node.hasAttribute(MARK);
  }

  new MutationObserver(function (records) {
    for (var i = 0; i < records.length; i++) {
      var r = records[i];
      /* Our own buttons going in must not schedule another scan. */
      var only = r.type === "childList" && r.removedNodes.length === 0 && Array.prototype.every.call(r.addedNodes, ours);
      if (!only) {
        soon();
        return;
      }
    }
  }).observe(document.body, { childList: true, subtree: true, characterData: true });
  window.addEventListener("routeLoaded", refresh);
  window.addEventListener("routeChangeEvent", refresh);
  refresh();
})();
</script>
```

</Accordion>

To call with the button:

1. Select the blue TalkChief button next to a number.
2. The TalkChief panel comes to the front and calls the number. If the panel wasn't open, it opens first. Allow pop-ups for your HighLevel address (app.gohighlevel.com or your own domain) when your browser asks.
3. TalkChief rings your TalkChief phone first. Answer it, and TalkChief dials the number.
4. When you called from a contact, the call is logged on that contact.

If the panel asks you to sign in, sign in, then select **Call** followed by the number in the panel. On your own white-label domain, a panel that just opened also waits for you to select **Call** with the number.

Good to know:

- **Only your connected sub-account:** the panel calls only for the sub-account it was opened in. In another sub-account, the button shows "The TalkChief panel is open for another business account" and doesn't call. Open the panel again from that sub-account's **TalkChief** menu.
- **Every sub-account sees the buttons:** the script is set for the whole agency, so the buttons appear in every sub-account. Sub-accounts that aren't connected to TalkChief can't call with them.
- **Nothing else is shared:** the script holds no keys or passwords. It sends nothing to TalkChief except the number you select, and only when you select it.
- **If HighLevel changes its pages,** the buttons may disappear until TalkChief updates the script. The panel's dial pad keeps working.

<Note>
  HighLevel's own call buttons on a contact still use HighLevel's phone system, not TalkChief: place TalkChief calls from the panel or with the TalkChief call button (see [Click a number to call](#click-a-number-to-call)). Your TalkChief phone must be online to place calls: keep the TalkChief app open or your desk phone connected. Your company admin must have connected HighLevel first (see [Connect HighLevel](#connect-highlevel)).
</Note>

## Data TalkChief uses in HighLevel

When you install the app, you allow TalkChief to:

| Access | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Read contacts | Find the contact whose phone number matches the call |
| Create and update contacts | Create a contact for a number HighLevel doesn't have (only while **Create leads for new numbers** is on), add the call note, and add the AI call summary to that note (only while **Add the AI call summary to each call** is on). TalkChief changes only the notes it wrote |
| Read and create conversations | Find the contact's conversation, or start one |
| Write conversation messages | Add the call entry with its recording link |
| Read users | Match the TalkChief agent to their HighLevel user by email |
| Read the sub-account | Show the connected sub-account's name in TalkChief |

TalkChief writes call entries, notes and new contacts to HighLevel. It does not read or change your opportunities, calendars, campaigns or other records, does not send messages to your contacts, and keeps no copy of your HighLevel records: only the connected sub-account's id and name, and the record ids it needs for a short time (at most 6 hours).

## Disconnect HighLevel

To stop logging calls, open **Integrations** in TalkChief and select the trash icon on the HighLevel card (or **Disconnect** in the **HighLevel settings** window), then confirm with **Yes, disconnect**.

When you disconnect:

- Calls are no longer logged in HighLevel. Calls that were waiting to be logged are dropped.
- TalkChief deletes its access tokens for your sub-account.
- Calls, notes and contacts already in HighLevel stay there. Nothing is deleted from HighLevel.

The TalkChief app stays installed in your HighLevel sub-account until you uninstall it there (see below). You can connect again at any time from the HighLevel card.

## Uninstall the app from HighLevel

To remove TalkChief completely, uninstall the TalkChief app from the sub-account in HighLevel. After you uninstall, calls stop being logged and the HighLevel card in TalkChief shows **Reconnect needed**. Calls, notes and contacts already in HighLevel stay there. To remove the connection from TalkChief too, disconnect it on the HighLevel card.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The HighLevel card asks me to reconnect">
    The card shows **Reconnect needed** when TalkChief lost access to HighLevel, usually because the app was uninstalled from the sub-account. Select **Reconnect** and approve access again. Calls that ended in the meantime are logged once you reconnect, as long as you reconnect within 7 days.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="TalkChief says to install in one business account">
    HighLevel installed the app for the agency without installing it in any sub-account, so TalkChief has no sub-account to log calls into. Select **Connect** again and, on HighLevel's page, choose at least one sub-account for the app. If it still fails, contact TalkChief support.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="TalkChief asks me to choose a business account">
    You installed the app from the agency into several sub-accounts. Choose the sub-account whose calls this TalkChief company should log. To log calls for another sub-account, connect it from that sub-account's own TalkChief company.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="This sub-account is already connected to another company">
    A HighLevel sub-account can be connected to only one TalkChief company, so that each company's calls stay in its own CRM. Disconnect it from the other TalkChief company first, or connect a different sub-account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A call is missing">
    TalkChief logs a call only on a contact with the caller's number (or the number you dialed). Check that the contact has that number. If **Create leads for new numbers** is off, calls from numbers HighLevel doesn't have are not logged.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The note has no agent">
    The note is written by the agent's HighLevel user only when the agent's TalkChief email matches a HighLevel user's email in the sub-account. Use the same email in both.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The panel doesn't open the caller's contact">
    Your browser is blocking pop-ups from app.talkchief.io. Allow them in your browser's settings, or select **Open record** in the panel for each call. The contact opens only when a contact in the connected sub-account has the caller's number.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I don't see the TalkChief call button">
    An agency admin must add TalkChief's click-to-dial script to the agency's **Custom JS** (see [Click a number to call](#click-a-number-to-call)). Then reload HighLevel. The buttons appear only inside a sub-account, never in **Agency View**, and only next to numbers HighLevel shows in the contact list, on a contact or in its conversation header.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The TalkChief call button doesn't open the panel">
    Your browser is blocking pop-ups from your HighLevel address. Allow pop-ups for app.gohighlevel.com, or for your own domain if your agency uses one, and select the button again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The panel is open for another business account">
    The panel was opened in a different sub-account than the one you're calling from. Close it, then open it again from this sub-account's **TalkChief** menu, and select the button again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The panel says my TalkChief phone is offline">
    TalkChief rings your own TalkChief phone first, so it must be registered: open the TalkChief app on your computer or phone, or check that your desk phone is connected, then try again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The panel asks me to sign in again">
    Your TalkChief sign-in in the panel lasts 30 days and renews by itself while you use the panel. You also need to sign in again after you change your TalkChief password. Select **Sign in** to continue.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A call's note has no AI summary">
    Check that **Add the AI call summary to each call** is on in the **HighLevel settings** window. The switch appears only when your company has [AI transcription](/ai/ai-transcription). Only calls that AI transcription covers get a summary; short calls, internal calls and calls from before it was turned on are not transcribed. The summary is added after the call has been transcribed, so it can appear a while after the call itself. It's not added if TalkChief's note on the contact was deleted.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A call has no recording">
    The recording link is added only when the call was recorded. Check that call recording is on for that number or team in TalkChief.
  </Accordion>
