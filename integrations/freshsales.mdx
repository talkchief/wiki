TalkChief logs every TalkChief phone call in your Freshsales Suite account without anyone typing it in. You connect once with your Freshworks login, with no API keys to copy, and from then on each finished call appears on the matching Freshsales contact as a phone call and a Phone sales activity, with the outcome, talk time, the agent who handled it and a link to the recording. With the TalkChief app added to Freshsales, agents can also click a contact's number in Freshsales and call it with TalkChief (see [Call from Freshsales](#call-from-freshsales)).

## What TalkChief does in Freshsales

- **Logs every external call**: inbound and outbound calls are logged. Calls between two TalkChief extensions are not logged.
- **Two records per call**: a phone call on the contact's timeline, and a sales activity of your account's **Phone** type with the call's start and end time, owner and outcome.
- **Finds the right contact**: TalkChief looks up the caller's number (or the number you dialed) in the **Mobile** and **Work** phone numbers of your Freshsales contacts. Freshsales matches a number only as it is written in the contact, so TalkChief tries the usual ways of writing it: international (`+13024370518`), national (`(302) 437-0518`), national digits only (`3024370518`) and international digits only (`13024370518`). When several contacts have the number, the call goes on the one updated most recently.
- **Creates contacts for new numbers**: when no contact has the number, TalkChief creates one with the number as its mobile number and the caller ID name as its name (or the number, when there is no name). When your account has a lifecycle stage named **Lead**, the new contact gets that stage. This is off until you turn it on (see [Choose what gets logged](#choose-what-gets-logged)); while it is off, calls from numbers Freshsales doesn't have are not logged.
- **Assigns the activity to the agent**: the sales activity's owner is the Freshsales user whose email matches the TalkChief agent's email. When no user matches, the contact's owner is used.
- **Adds the recording**: when the call was recorded, the notes of both records include a link to the recording.
- **Adds the AI call summary, if you want**: with [AI transcription](/ai/ai-transcription) on, TalkChief can add the call's AI summary to the notes of the call's Phone sales activity once it's ready, with a link to the full transcript. This is off until you turn it on (see [Choose what gets logged](#choose-what-gets-logged)).

Each logged call shows:

| Freshsales record | What TalkChief puts there |
| --- | --- |
| Phone call | Direction (incoming or outgoing), the outcome, and a note with the details below |
| Phone sales activity | Title (**Inbound call from** or **Outbound call to**, followed by the other party's number), start and end time, owner, outcome and the same notes |
| Notes | The title, the call status, the agent's name and extension, talk time, the start time (UTC), the recording link when there is one, and **Logged by TalkChief** |
| Outcome | Your account's own Phone outcome with a matching name: **Connected** (or **Interested**) for answered calls, **Left voicemail**, **Busy**, or **No answer**. When your account has no outcome with a matching name, the call is logged without one |

With **Add the AI call summary to each call** on, the summary is added at the end of the Phone sales activity's notes after the call has been transcribed:

```text
[AI summary]
The customer asked to move their delivery to Friday. The agent confirmed the new date and will send a confirmation email.

Full transcript in TalkChief: https://...
[End of AI summary]
```

Calls usually appear in Freshsales within a few minutes of hanging up.

## Before you start

- You are a **company admin** in TalkChief.
- You use **Freshsales Suite**: the web address you see when you are signed in to Freshsales ends with **myfreshworks.com**. Freshsales Classic is not supported.
- You connect with a Freshsales user who can see and create the contacts and sales activities TalkChief works with. TalkChief sees only what that user can see in Freshsales, so connecting as an admin is the simplest choice.
- One Freshsales account can be connected to one TalkChief company.

## Connect Freshsales

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations in TalkChief">
    In the TalkChief web app, select **Integrations** under **Configuration** in the sidebar. The page opens on the **CRM** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the connection">
    On the **Freshsales** card, select **Connect**. The **Connect Freshsales** window opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your Freshsales address">
    In **Freshsales address**, enter the first part of the web address you see when you are signed in to Freshsales, for example `yourcompany.myfreshworks.com`. You can also paste the whole address from your browser. Fill in **Organization URL (optional)** only when your Freshworks organization URL is different from your Freshsales address. Select **Connect**. TalkChief sends you to Freshworks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and choose the account">
    Sign in with your Freshworks login. If your login has more than one account, choose the account with the address you entered, and select **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve access">
    Freshworks shows the access TalkChief asks for (see [Data TalkChief uses in Freshsales](#data-talkchief-uses-in-freshsales)). Select **Agree**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm in TalkChief">
    Freshworks sends you back to **Integrations** in TalkChief. A message confirms that Freshsales is connected, and the Freshsales card moves to your connected integrations with a **Connected** label.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test with a real call">
    Make or receive a call with TalkChief. A few minutes after you hang up, open the contact in Freshsales and check that the phone call and the Phone sales activity are on it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose what gets logged

On the connected Freshsales card in **Integrations**, select the settings (gear) icon. The **Freshsales settings** window shows the connected Freshsales account and the switches you can change at any time. Each change is saved as soon as you flip the switch and applies to calls that end afterwards.

| Setting | Default | On | Off |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Log calls** | On | Every external call is logged | Nothing is logged, and the connection stays in place. The other switches are unavailable while this is off |
| **Log missed calls** | On | Busy, unanswered, cancelled and voicemail calls are logged too | Only answered calls are logged |
| **Create leads for new numbers** | Off | Unknown numbers get a new Freshsales contact, so their calls are logged too | Calls from numbers Freshsales doesn't have are not logged, because Freshsales logs calls only on a record |
| **Add the AI call summary to each call** | Off | When a call's AI summary is ready, it's added to the notes of the call's Phone sales activity, with a link to the full transcript | Calls are logged without a summary |

If your company doesn't have [AI transcription](/ai/ai-transcription) yet, **Add the AI call summary to each call** is locked and shows **Needs AI transcription.** Select **Turn it on** next to it to open **Settings** > **AI transcription**, turn it on there, and then come back and turn on the switch.

The summary goes in the Phone sales activity's notes, next to the call details, and is added only to calls that AI transcription covers. If a summary is created again, TalkChief replaces its earlier summary in the notes instead of adding a second one. Everything else in the notes, including text your team typed in Freshsales, stays as it is.

<Tip>
  Turn on **Create leads for new numbers** if you want every call in Freshsales, including calls from people who are not contacts yet.
</Tip>

## Call from Freshsales

The TalkChief app adds a TalkChief panel to Freshsales, so each agent can call a contact by clicking their number. Calls are placed in the **TalkChief phone window**, a small TalkChief window of its own next to Freshsales: you talk in your browser there, or on your usual TalkChief phone.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the TalkChief app to Freshsales">
    The TalkChief app for Freshsales is installed from a link made for your Freshsales account. Open **Integrations** in TalkChief. On the connected Freshsales card, select **Install TalkChief in Freshsales**: Freshworks opens in a new tab, where a Freshsales admin follows the steps to install it. If the card shows **Your TalkChief team is preparing your install link**, your link isn't ready yet: check again later, or contact TalkChief support. Once installed, the TalkChief icon appears at the bottom of the left sidebar in Freshsales.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to TalkChief">
    Select the TalkChief icon in the Freshsales sidebar, then select **Sign in**. A TalkChief window opens; sign in if asked, and it closes by itself when you are connected. If nothing opens, allow pop-ups for Freshsales in your browser. Each agent signs in once on their own computer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the number to call">
    Click a contact's **Mobile** or **Work** number in Freshsales. The TalkChief panel opens with the number in its dial pad. Nothing is dialed yet. You can also type a number in the panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Call in the TalkChief phone window">
    Select **Call in TalkChief phone**. The TalkChief phone window opens with the same number. Select **Call** there. The first time, allow the microphone for TalkChief when your browser asks. Keep the TalkChief phone window open while you work: when it's open, a number you click in Freshsales goes straight to its dial pad, and the panel shows **Calls ring in the TalkChief window**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive a call">
    While the TalkChief phone window is open, calls to you ring there, with **Accept** and **Decline**, and your other TalkChief phones ring too. Otherwise they ring on your TalkChief phone as usual (the TalkChief app or your desk phone). When the caller's number is on a Freshsales contact, the window shows the contact's name; select **Open record** to open the contact in Freshsales in a new tab. Freshsales doesn't open the contact by itself. The contact is found only when the number saved in Freshsales is the caller's full phone number (see [What TalkChief does in Freshsales](#what-talkchief-does-in-freshsales)), so short internal numbers such as extensions don't match.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Only the TalkChief phone window places calls, and only when you select **Call** in it. Clicking a number in Freshsales never starts a call by itself, and no other app in Freshsales can start one for you.
</Note>

### Add a note after the call

When a call ends, the TalkChief phone window shows **Call logged**. Pick an outcome (**Connected**, **Left voicemail**, **No answer**, **Busy**, **Wrong number** or **Other**), type a note if you want, and select **Save note**. TalkChief adds the note to the notes of the call's Phone sales activity, between **[Agent note]** and **[End of agent note]**. When your account has a Phone outcome with the same name, the activity gets that outcome. You can change the note later; TalkChief replaces only its own note, and text your team typed in Freshsales stays as it is.

## Data TalkChief uses in Freshsales

When you connect, you allow TalkChief to:

| Access | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Read contacts | Find the contact whose mobile or work number matches the call |
| Create contacts | Create a contact for a number Freshsales doesn't have (only while **Create leads for new numbers** is on) |
| Read users | Match the TalkChief agent to their Freshsales user by email, to set the activity's owner |
| Read sales activity types, outcomes and lifecycle stages | Find your **Phone** activity type, the outcome that matches the call, and the **Lead** stage for new contacts |
| Create phone calls and sales activities | Log each call on the contact |
| Read and update sales activities | Add the AI call summary to the notes of the call's Phone sales activity (only while **Add the AI call summary to each call** is on). TalkChief changes only its own summary there |

TalkChief writes phone calls, Phone sales activities and new contacts to Freshsales. It does not read or change your deals, accounts, tasks, emails or other records, and it does not copy your Freshsales contacts into TalkChief.

## Disconnect Freshsales

To stop logging calls, open **Integrations** in TalkChief and select the trash icon on the Freshsales card (or **Disconnect** in the **Freshsales settings** window), then confirm with **Yes, disconnect**.

When you disconnect:

- Calls are no longer logged in Freshsales. Calls that were waiting to be logged are dropped.
- TalkChief deletes the access it stored for your Freshsales account.
- Calls, activities and contacts already in Freshsales stay there. Nothing is deleted from Freshsales.

You can connect again at any time: the Freshsales card returns to **Discover more integrations**, where you select **Connect**.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The Freshsales card asks me to reconnect">
    The card shows **Reconnect needed** when Freshworks no longer accepts TalkChief's access, for example because the access expired or the Freshsales user who connected was removed from the account. Select **Reconnect** (on the card or in the **Freshsales settings** window); the **Connect Freshsales** window opens with your address filled in. Approve access again. Calls that ended in the meantime are logged once you reconnect, as long as you reconnect within 7 days.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The Connect Freshsales window says the address is not valid">
    Enter the address of a Freshsales Suite account: it ends with **myfreshworks.com** (or **freshworks.com** for an organization URL), for example `yourcompany.myfreshworks.com`. Freshsales Classic addresses are not supported.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="TalkChief says it could not connect Freshsales">
    TalkChief checks that the access you approved opens the Freshsales address you entered. Make sure you chose that same account on the Freshworks screen, then connect again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="This Freshsales account is already connected to another company">
    A Freshsales account can be connected to only one TalkChief company, so that each company's calls stay in its own CRM. Disconnect it from the other TalkChief company first, or connect a different Freshsales account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A call is not in Freshsales">
    TalkChief matches the contact by its **Mobile** or **Work** phone number. Check that the contact has the caller's number in one of those fields. **Create leads for new numbers** is off by default; while it is off, calls from numbers Freshsales doesn't have are not logged.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The activity is owned by someone other than the agent">
    The owner is the agent's Freshsales user only when the agent's TalkChief email matches a Freshsales user's email; otherwise the contact's owner is used. Use the same email in both, or add the agent as a user in Freshsales.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A call has no outcome">
    TalkChief picks the Phone outcome whose name matches the call: **Connected** (or **Interested**), **Left voicemail**, **Busy** or **No answer**. If your account uses other names, the call is logged without an outcome.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A recording link is missing">
    The recording link is added only when the call was recorded. Check that call recording is on for that number or team in TalkChief.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A call has no AI summary">
    Check that **Add the AI call summary to each call** is on in the **Freshsales settings** window. If the switch is locked, your company needs [AI transcription](/ai/ai-transcription) first. Only calls that AI transcription covers get a summary; short calls, internal calls and calls from before it was turned on are not transcribed. The summary is added after the call has been transcribed, so it can appear a while after the call itself. Look for it in the notes of the call's Phone sales activity, not on the phone call. It's not added if the Phone sales activity was deleted, or if the call was logged without one because your account has no **Phone** activity type.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A call was logged on a new contact instead of the existing one">
    Freshsales finds a number only as it is written in the contact. TalkChief tries the international form (`+13024370518`), the national form (`(302) 437-0518`) and both as digits only. A number written another way, such as `302.437.0518` or `+1 302 437 0518`, isn't found, so with **Create leads for new numbers** on, TalkChief creates a new contact. Write the contact's number in one of the forms above, then merge the two contacts in Freshsales.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Call in TalkChief phone opens nothing">
    Your browser blocked the TalkChief phone window. Allow pop-ups for Freshsales, then select **Call in TalkChief phone** again. If the window opened behind Freshsales, bring it to the front from your taskbar.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I don't see the TalkChief icon in Freshsales">
    The TalkChief app must be installed in your Freshsales account (see [Call from Freshsales](#call-from-freshsales)), and enabled in Freshsales under **Admin Settings** > **Apps** > **Manage Apps** > **Custom Apps**. Reload Freshsales after installing it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Calls appear late on a very busy account">
    Freshsales limits how many API requests an account can make each hour (1,000 on the Free and Growth plans, 2,000 on Pro and 5,000 on Enterprise). Each call takes about four requests. When the limit is reached, TalkChief waits and logs the calls later.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

For anything else, contact TalkChief support at [support@talkchief.io](mailto:support@talkchief.io).
