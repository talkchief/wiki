TalkChief gives you two views of your call data under **Insights**. **Dashboards** show totals, rates, and trends. **Reports** list every call, one row each, with its recording. This page is for admins and supervisors.

<Note>
  Admins see data for the whole company. Supervisors see only their own and supervised teams. Agents do not have access to Insights.
</Note>

## Dashboards

Go to **Insights** > **Dashboards**. Pick a tab, set the filters, and select **Apply**. The default range is **This Month**.

| Tab | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Outbound** | **Total outbound calls**, **Total duration**, **Avg call duration**, **Answer rate**, calls per member and team, volume over time, **Calls by hour of day**, **Talk-time distribution** |
| **Inbound** | **Total inbound calls**, **Avg wait time**, **Answer rate**, **Missed calls**, **Total cost**, **Missed-call recovery**, **First-contact resolution**, **Repeat-caller rate**, **Top DIDs by missed-call %**, **Top repeat callers** |
| **Queues** | **Total queued calls**, **Avg wait time**, **Longest wait**, **Abandoned calls**, **Service level (SLA)**, **Avg handle time**, **Queue outcome**, **Abandoned calls by hour** |
| **Billing** | **Subscriptions cost**, **Calls cost**, **DIDs cost**, **Total cost**, **Cost per team**, **Cost per member**, **DID cost per number**, **Monthly cost trend**, **Monthly breakdown** |
| **AI insights** | Sentiment, resolution, escalations, call reasons, and team and member scorecards. See [AI insights](/ai/ai-insights). |

Each chart has an info icon that explains how it is calculated.

### Dashboard filters

- **Range**: a preset such as **Today**, **This Week**, **Last Month**, **Last 30 days**, or **Custom**.
- **From** and **To**: exact dates for a custom range.
- **Team** (or **Queue** on the **Queues** tab) and **Member**.

Select **Reset** to go back to the defaults.

## Reports

Go to **Insights** > **Reports**. Pick the **Outbound**, **Inbound**, or **Queue** tab. The default range is **Today**.

Each row is one call, with these columns:

| Column | Shows |
|---|---|
| **DATE** | When the call started |
| **TEAM** and **Member** | Who handled the call |
| **CALLER ID** and **CALL TO** | The two numbers |
| **RING TIME** and **TALK TIME** | How long it rang and how long the parties talked |
| **CALL STATUS** | **Answered**, **No Answer**, **Busy**, or **Failed** |
| **CALL COST** | The cost of the call |
| **RECORDING** | Play the recording, open the AI transcript, and see an escalation badge |
| **ACTION** | Where the call went, for example "Forwarded to" a number |

### Find a call

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the report">
    Go to **Insights** > **Reports** and pick **Outbound**, **Inbound**, or **Queue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the filters">
    Choose a **Team**, a **Member**, and a **Range**. Filter by call status, or type a number or name in the search box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply">
    Select **Apply**. Use **Rows per page** and the page controls to browse the results.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Recordings and transcripts

In the **RECORDING** column:

- Select play to listen to the call.
- Select **Open transcript** to read the call as a conversation and see its summary. This appears only for calls transcribed by [AI transcription](/ai/ai-transcription).
- A **High**, **Medium**, or **Low** badge marks an escalated call. Select it to open the transcript at the escalation line.

See [Transcripts and summaries](/ai/transcripts-and-summaries) for details.

## Find calls by what was said

Reports filter by numbers, names, and status. To find calls by the words spoken, use [Call search](/ai/call-search).

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why do I see fewer calls than a colleague?">
    Supervisors see only their own and supervised teams. Admins see the whole company.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is there no transcript icon on some calls?">
    Only calls covered by AI transcription have transcripts. Short calls, internal calls, and calls from before it was turned on are not transcribed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I watch calls that are happening now?">
    Use **Live Calls** for active calls. See [Live calls](/contact-center/monitoring-and-coaching).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
