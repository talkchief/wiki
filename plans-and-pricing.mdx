TalkChief uses a straightforward per-member, per-month pricing model so your costs scale directly with your team. Every plan includes the core unified communications platform — business phone, Cowork, and analytics — with higher tiers unlocking advanced contact-center features, dedicated support, and real-time SLAs. You can pay month-to-month for maximum flexibility or choose annual billing to save up to 15%.

## Available Plans

| Plan | Annual (per member/mo) | Monthly (per member/mo) | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Essential** | $8 | $9 | Small teams that need a reliable cloud phone system |
| **Growth** | $13 | $15 | Growing teams that want interactive dashboards and guided onboarding |
| **Professional** | $17 | $20 | Teams that require a dedicated account manager, real-time dashboards, and an SLA |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Custom | Large organizations needing tailored contracts, onboarding, and infrastructure |

## Billing and Savings

Choosing **annual billing** locks in your rate and saves you up to **15%** compared to paying month-to-month. You can switch between monthly and annual billing at any time from your workspace **Settings → Billing** page.

## AI Transcription Add-On

TalkChief's AI Transcription add-on delivers diarized, speaker-labeled transcripts in Arabic, Hebrew, and English.

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| **Add-on price** | $10 per member/month |
| **Included minutes** | 1,500 minutes per member/month |
| **Overage rate** | $0.025 per minute |
| **Minimum billing unit** | 1 minute, then per-second proration |
| **Failed transcription jobs** | Not billed |

<Info>
  Per-second proration kicks in after the first full minute. A 75-second call is billed as 1 minute + 15 seconds, not rounded up to 2 minutes. Jobs that fail to produce a transcript are never charged.
</Info>
