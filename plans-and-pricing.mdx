TalkChief is billed per member. You pick a plan for your workspace, then add optional services such as AI transcription or AI voice agents. This page is for admins who choose plans and manage billing.

<Note>
  Plans and billing live in **Settings**, which is part of the admin workspace. Only admins can turn on the AI transcription add-on. Agents use a separate agent workspace without billing pages.
</Note>

## Plans

TalkChief offers three self-serve plans and a custom option for larger companies.

| Plan | Who it suits | How to get it |
|---|---|---|
| **Essential** | Teams that need a cloud business phone | Self-serve in **Settings** > **Plans** |
| **Growth** | Teams that also want AI transcription and call insights | Self-serve in **Settings** > **Plans** |
| **Professional** | Teams that want the full self-serve feature set | Self-serve in **Settings** > **Plans** |
| **Enterprise** | Larger companies that need custom terms or on-demand add-ons such as Power Dialer | [Contact sales](https://talkchief.io/en/contact) |

Current prices are shown on each plan card in **Settings** > **Plans** and on the [TalkChief pricing page](https://talkchief.io/en/Pricing). Some limits, such as the number of call queues you can create, depend on your plan.

<Tip>
  Switch the toggle at the top of the **Plans** page between **Monthly** and **Yearly** to compare prices. When yearly billing costs less, each card shows how much you save versus monthly.
</Tip>

## Change your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Plans page">
    In the sidebar, go to **Configuration** > **Settings**, then select **Plans**. The header shows your current plan and the next renewal date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a billing cycle">
    Select **Monthly** or **Yearly**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Select **Choose** followed by the plan name, for example **Choose Growth**. A **Plan change requested** message confirms the request. Your current plan is marked **Current plan**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  ![Settings > Plans with the Monthly/Yearly toggle, three plan cards with one marked Current plan, and the AI Call Intelligence add-on card below them](/images/docs/settings-plans.png)
</Frame>

To see a full comparison of what each plan includes, select **Compare plans** on the same page.

## Free trial

New companies start with a 7-day free trial. Sign up at [talkchief.io/register](https://talkchief.io/register/). During the trial, the list of countries you can buy numbers in is limited. The full list opens after you upgrade.

When the trial ends, TalkChief asks you to pick a plan in **Settings** > **Plans** to keep using your workspace.

## AI transcription add-on

AI transcription turns your recorded calls into text, then adds summaries, search, and escalation alerts. On the **Plans** page it appears as the **AI Call Intelligence add-on**. You turn it on in **Settings** > **AI transcription**.

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Plans | **Growth** and **Professional**, including trials |
| Price | $10 per member per month, or $120 per member per year on yearly plans |
| Included minutes | 1,500 minutes per member each month |
| Overage | $0.01 per minute beyond the included minutes, charged from your wallet and prorated per second |
| First month | The fee is prorated from the day you turn it on. The full 1,500 minutes apply right away. |
| Short calls | Calls shorter than your minimum call duration (0 to 600 seconds) are skipped and never charged |
| Internal calls | Calls between members are never transcribed |
| Requirement | **Auto Top-up** must be on in your wallet before you can enable the add-on |

<Warning>
  Calls are transcribed only from the moment you turn AI transcription on. Earlier calls are not transcribed.
</Warning>

If you turn the add-on off, coverage continues until the end of the period you already paid for. For setup steps, see [AI transcription](/ai/ai-transcription).

## AI voice agents

AI voice agents answer and place calls for you. You build and manage them at [agents.talkchief.io](https://agents.talkchief.io), which opens from **AI Voice Agents** in the sidebar.

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Price | $0.15 per connected minute, or the usage-based cost of the call if that is higher |
| Billing | Charged from your wallet |
| Minimum balance | Your wallet must hold at least $10 for an AI voice agent call to start |

<Tip>
  Turn on **Auto Top-up** in **Settings** > **Wallet** so AI voice agent calls and transcription overage never stop because of a low balance.
</Tip>

## Power Dialer

Power Dialer is an on-demand add-on for Enterprise companies. It is not available as a self-serve option. See [Power Dialer](/contact-center/power-dialer) or [contact sales](https://talkchief.io/en/contact).

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I use AI transcription on the Essential plan?">
    No. AI transcription is available on the **Growth** and **Professional** plans, including trials. Upgrade in **Settings** > **Plans** first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do unused AI transcription minutes roll over?">
    No. Each member gets 1,500 included minutes per calendar month. Minutes beyond that cost $0.01 per minute from your wallet.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did my AI voice agent call not start?">
    AI voice agent calls need a wallet balance of at least $10. Top up your wallet in **Settings** > **Wallet**, or turn on **Auto Top-up**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I get Power Dialer?">
    Power Dialer is available on request for Enterprise companies. [Contact sales](https://talkchief.io/en/contact) to discuss it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
