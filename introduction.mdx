Whether you're a growing startup routing your first calls or an enterprise managing multilingual support queues across continents, TalkChief gives your team the tools to communicate faster, smarter, and with full visibility.

## Get Up and Running

Follow these three steps to go from zero to a fully operational TalkChief workspace.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start Your Free Trial">
    Visit [talkchief.io/en/register](https://talkchief.io/en/register) to create your account. No credit card is required — your 7-day free trial begins immediately so you can explore every feature before committing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Up Your Phone Number and Routing">
    Choose a local, toll-free, or international number from 90+ countries. Then configure your IVR menus, ring groups, and business hours so every call reaches the right person at the right time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite Your Team">
    Add team members to your workspace, assign them to queues or ring groups, and enable Cowork channels so your entire organization is connected from day one.
  </Step>
</Steps>
