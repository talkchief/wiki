TalkChief is a cloud business phone and contact center for teams. You buy numbers, route calls to teams and queues, watch live calls, and review every call afterward. AI transcription and AI voice agents add text, insights, and automated calling on top. This page is for anyone new to TalkChief who wants a map of the product.

<Note>
  Admins see the full workspace described below. Agents sign in to a smaller agent workspace with their own **Dashboard**, **Queues**, and **Settings**. Some features depend on your plan. See [Plans and pricing](/plans-and-pricing).
</Note>

## How the workspace is organized

The sidebar groups every page into five areas.

| Sidebar group | Pages | What you do there |
|---|---|---|
| **Workspace** | **Dashboard** | See call activity, team performance, and download the apps |
| **Calls** | **Numbers**, **Live Calls**, **Voicemail**, **AI Voice Agents** | Buy and route numbers, monitor calls in progress, listen to voicemail, and open the AI voice agents app |
| **Team** | **Teams & Members**, **Queues** | Invite members, organize teams, and build call queues |
| **Insights** | **Dashboards**, **Reports**, **Call search**, **Escalations** | Analyze calls, read transcripts, search conversations, and handle AI escalations |
| **Configuration** | **Media**, **Integrations**, **Settings** | Upload greetings and hold music, connect your CRM, and manage billing |

## AI voice agents

AI voice agents answer inbound calls and run outbound calling campaigns for you. You build them in a separate app at [agents.talkchief.io](https://agents.talkchief.io). Select **AI Voice Agents** in the sidebar to open it in a new tab. To send inbound calls to an agent, add an **AI Agent** step to a number's call flow.

AI voice agents cost $0.15 per connected minute, or the usage-based cost if that is higher, charged from your wallet. See [Plans and pricing](/plans-and-pricing).

## AI transcription

AI transcription turns recorded calls into a conversation you can read, search, and summarize. It also flags calls that may need a supervisor in **Escalations**. It is an add-on for the **Growth** and **Professional** plans. See [AI transcription](/ai/ai-transcription).

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Create your company at [talkchief.io/register](https://talkchief.io/register/). Every new company starts with a 7-day free trial.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete onboarding">
    Enter your company information and working hours, then pick your first number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team and route calls">
    Add members in **Teams & Members**, then set up **Queues** and call flows.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For the detailed walkthrough, see the [Quickstart](/quickstart).

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to install anything?">
    No. You can make and receive calls in the browser with the **Web Phone**. Desktop apps for Windows and macOS and mobile apps for iOS and Android are also available.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I build AI voice agents?">
    At [agents.talkchief.io](https://agents.talkchief.io). Open it from **AI Voice Agents** in the sidebar.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which CRMs does TalkChief connect to?">
    HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho, Zoho PhoneBridge, Powerlink, and Comeunity. Connect them in **Configuration** > **Integrations**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
