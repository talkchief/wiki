This guide takes you from a new account to your first call. It is for the admin who sets up the company workspace. You will finish onboarding, get a number, invite your team, and place a call.

<Note>
  Every new company starts with a 7-day free trial. During the trial, the list of countries you can buy numbers in is limited. The full list opens after you choose a plan in **Settings** > **Plans**.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [talkchief.io/register](https://talkchief.io/register/) and sign up. After you confirm your details, sign in to your new workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your company information">
    The onboarding wizard opens on first sign-in. Under **Complete your company information**, enter your **Company Name**, industry, website, **Time Zone**, address, **Billing Email**, **Invoice Email Address**, and **Primary Contact Number**. Set your **Working Hours** and pick at least one working day, then select **Continue**.

    <Frame>
  ![Onboarding wizard step 1, Complete your company information, with demo company fields and working hours filled in](/images/docs/onboarding-company-info.png)
</Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your first number">
    Answer **In which country would you like your first number?** by choosing a country, state if shown, and city. Your number is assigned to your first team. Select **Start making calls**.

    <Frame>
  ![Onboarding wizard step 2 with a country and city selected and a number available, plus the Start making calls button](/images/docs/onboarding-first-number.png)
</Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the apps (optional)">
    On **Now let's download your phone apps**, download the desktop app for Windows or macOS, or scan the code to get the mobile app for iOS or Android. You can select **Skip** and use the browser instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    In the sidebar, go to **Team** > **Teams & Members**. Select **Invite member** to add people, and **+ Add Team** to group them. Adding a member can add a charge to your bill, so you confirm each invite with **Pay and Invite**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your first call">
    Open the **Web Phone** from the bottom corner of any page. Type a number in **Enter number** and select **Call**. You can also use the **Recents** and **Contacts** tabs. Your browser asks for microphone access the first time.

    <Frame>
  ![Web Phone panel open on the Keypad tab with a demo number typed in and the Call button visible](/images/docs/web-phone-keypad.png)
</Frame>

    <Tip>
      Call your own mobile first to check audio and confirm the caller ID your customers will see.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set up routing next

After your first call, decide how inbound calls reach your team.

| Task | Where |
|---|---|
| Buy more numbers | **Calls** > **Numbers** > **Buy number** |
| Edit what happens when a number is called | **Calls** > **Numbers**, then **Callflow** on the number |
| Build a call queue | **Team** > **Queues** > **+ New Queue** |
| Upload greetings and hold music | **Configuration** > **Media** |
| Connect your CRM | **Configuration** > **Integrations** |
| Turn on AI transcription | **Configuration** > **Settings** > **AI transcription** |
| Build an AI voice agent | **Calls** > **AI Voice Agents** (opens agents.talkchief.io) |

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can I only see a few countries when buying a number?">
    During the free trial the country list is limited. Choose a plan in **Settings** > **Plans** to see the full list.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I skip downloading the apps?">
    Yes. Select **Skip** in onboarding and use the **Web Phone** in your browser. You can download the apps later from the **Dashboard**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do agents sign in?">
    Agents sign in at the same address. TalkChief opens the agent workspace for them, with their own **Dashboard**, **Queues**, and **Settings**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
