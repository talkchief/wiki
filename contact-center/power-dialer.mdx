Power Dialer helps teams that make a high volume of outbound calls work through their call lists faster. It is an on-demand add-on that TalkChief sets up for Enterprise companies. This page is for managers who want to know whether Power Dialer fits their team and how to request it.

<Note>
  Power Dialer is available on request for **Enterprise** companies only. It is not part of the **Essential**, **Growth**, or **Professional** plans and cannot be turned on from **Settings**.
</Note>

## What Power Dialer is for

Power Dialer is built for teams whose day is mostly outbound calls, for example:

- Sales teams following up on leads
- Customer success teams making check-in calls
- Operations teams confirming appointments or collecting feedback

The TalkChief team scopes and configures Power Dialer with you, so the setup matches your call lists, team, and process.

## How to get Power Dialer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Contact sales">
    Reach out to the TalkChief sales team through the [contact page](https://talkchief.io/en/contact). Tell us about your team size, call volume, and how you work today.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Agree on the setup">
    Our team confirms whether Power Dialer fits your needs and agrees the scope and pricing with you as part of your Enterprise agreement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live">
    TalkChief sets up Power Dialer for your company and helps your team get started.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Other ways to make outbound calls

If you do not need Power Dialer, you can still make outbound calls in every plan:

| Option | Best for |
|---|---|
| **Web Phone** | Members placing calls one at a time from the browser |
| TalkChief desktop and mobile apps | Members calling from their computer or phone |
| AI voice agent outbound campaigns | Calling a list of leads with an AI voice agent at [agents.talkchief.io](https://agents.talkchief.io) |
| Click-to-call API | Starting calls from your own software. See the [developer docs](/developers/overview). |

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I try Power Dialer during the free trial?">
    No. Power Dialer is set up on request for Enterprise companies. [Contact sales](https://talkchief.io/en/contact) to discuss it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How much does Power Dialer cost?">
    Pricing is agreed with the sales team as part of your Enterprise agreement.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is Power Dialer the same as AI voice agent campaigns?">
    No. Power Dialer helps your members make calls. AI voice agent campaigns let an AI voice agent place the calls. Campaigns are built at [agents.talkchief.io](https://agents.talkchief.io).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
