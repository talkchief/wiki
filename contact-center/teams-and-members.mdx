Teams group your members and numbers for routing, caller ID, and reporting. Manage both in **Team** → **Teams & Members**. The page has two tabs: **Teams** and **Members**.

<Note>
  Admins manage everything on this page. Supervisors can open it too, but some actions, such as **Assign to team** and **Delete Member**, are admin-only. Agents don't see this page.
</Note>

## Roles

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Admin | Full access, including numbers, billing settings, and deleting members. |
| Supervisor | Works in the admin area with their teams, including escalations. A supervisor is set per team. |
| Agent | Uses the [agent workspace](/contact-center/agent-workspace): **Dashboard**, **Queues**, and **Settings**. |

## Create a team

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new team">
    On the **Teams** tab, select **+ Add Team**. The **Create Team** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and set an extension">
    Enter a **Name**, such as "Support", and a **Team Extension**, such as `100`. Calling the team extension reaches all team members.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create">
    Select **Create**. The team appears as a card.
  </Step>
</Steps>

On a team card you can select **Manage Team** to open it, or use **Actions** to **Change Team Color** or **Delete Team**.

## Manage a team

Select **Manage Team** to open the team. It has four tabs.

| Tab | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **Members** | See **Supervisors** and **Members**. Select **Assign Members +** to add members. In a member's **Actions** menu, choose **Make Supervisor**, **Remove Supervisor**, or **Move to Team**. |
| **Numbers** | Select **Assign Numbers** to add numbers. Use **Make Default** or **Remove from this Team**. |
| **Outbound** | Set the team's caller ID. See [Numbers and caller ID](/phone/numbers-and-caller-id). |
| **Settings** | Change the **Team Extension** (2–6 digits), view the **Time Zone**, and set **Working Hours**. Select **Delete Team** to remove it. |

<Warning>
  Move every member to another team before you delete a team. Deleting a team can't be undone.
</Warning>

<Note>
  A member belongs to one team at a time. Use **Move to Team** to change it.
</Note>

## Invite a member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the invite">
    On the **Members** tab, select **Invite member**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter member details">
    Fill in **First name**, **Last name**, and **Work Email**. Choose the role under **User permissions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign a team">
    Under **Assign member to team**, pick the **Assigned Team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set call recording and extension">
    Turn **Call Recording** on to record **All Calls**, **Outbound Only**, or **Inbound Only**. Set the **Member extension**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invite">
    Review the summary and select **Pay and Invite**. The member gets an email to set up their account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Each member adds a monthly seat cost. The invite summary shows the amount before you confirm.
</Note>

## Manage members

The **Members** tab shows counts for **Total members**, **Active**, **Invite Sent**, and **Inactive**. Select a count to filter the list. Each row shows the **Member**, **Extension**, **Team**, **Role**, **Status**, **Devices**, and **Call Forward**.

The actions menu changes with the member's status:

| Status | Actions |
|---|---|
| **Active** | **Edit profile**, **Assign to team**, **Call Forward**, **Reset Password**, **Deactivate**, **Settings**, **Delete Member** |
| **Inactive** | **Assign to team**, **Reset Password**, **Activate**, **Delete Member** |
| **Invite Sent** | **Resend Invite**, **Get Invitation link**, **Cancel Invite** |

**Deactivate** blocks the member's sign-in but keeps all their settings. **Delete Member** removes the member and all related settings.

### Hand a seat to someone new

Use **Edit profile** to change a member's name. If you change the email, the seat goes to a new person. The extension, team, call flows, devices, and queues stay as they are. The old login stops working right away, and the new address gets an email to set a password. Select **Replace member** to confirm.

### Check a member's devices

The **Devices** column shows each registered phone or app as **Online**, **Offline**, or **Connectivity Issues**. Select a device to see its **Device Details**.

## Member settings

In a member's actions menu, select **Settings** to open their full settings page.

| Section | Settings |
|---|---|
| **Contact Info** | **First name**, **Last name**, **Email**, **Extension** (2–4 digits) |
| **Call Forward** | Forward to another number when unreachable. See [Call routing](/phone/call-routing). |
| **Role** | Change the role under **User permissions**. |
| **Team** | Change the **Assigned Team**. |
| **Call Recording** | **All Calls**, **Outbound Only**, or **Inbound Only**. Shows **Upgrade required** if your plan doesn't include it. |
| **Voicemail** | Mailbox options. See [Voicemail](/phone/voicemail). |

Select **Save changes** to apply, or **Discard** to undo. The **Actions** menu on this page also has **Clear Saved VMbox Messages**, **Reset Password**, **Resend invitation**, **Deactivate** or **Activate**, and **Remove from workspace**.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can a member be on two teams?">
    No. A member belongs to one team. Use **Move to Team** or **Assign to team** to change it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I make someone a supervisor?">
    Open the team with **Manage Team**, go to the **Members** tab, and select **Make Supervisor** in the member's **Actions** menu.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The invitee never got the email">
    Select **Resend Invite**. You can also select **Get Invitation link** and send it to them yourself.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
