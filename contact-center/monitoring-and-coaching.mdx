The **Live Calls** page shows every call in progress in your workspace. From it, you can listen to a call, coach the member on it, or join the conversation. This page is for admins and supervisors who look after call quality.

<Note>
  **Live Calls** is part of the admin workspace, so admins and supervisors can use it. Admins see every live call in the company. Supervisors see the calls of their own team. Agents do not have access.
</Note>

## Open Live Calls

In the sidebar, go to **Calls** > **Live Calls**.

The list refreshes on its own every few seconds. Each row shows:

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Member Name** | The member on the call |
| **Call From** | The number or extension that placed the call |
| **Call To** | The number or extension that was called |
| **Direction** | Whether the call is inbound or outbound |
| **Status** | Whether the call is ringing or answered |
| **Duration** | How long the call has been going |
| **Supervisor Actions** | The **Listen**, **Coach**, and **Join** buttons |

Use **Search** to filter the list by member, number, direction, or status. When no calls are in progress, the page shows **No Active Calls**.

## Choose how you connect

Before you monitor a call, choose where the monitoring call rings.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the settings menu">
    On the **Live Calls** page, select **Settings** next to the search box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a device">
    Under **Perform Actions using:**, choose **Web Phone** to take the monitoring call in your browser, or **Phone** to take it on your own TalkChief phone or app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Auto-Answer">
    Leave **Auto-Answer** on to connect straight away without answering the monitoring call yourself. Turn it off if you want to answer it manually. **Auto-Answer** is on by default.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  ![Live Calls Settings menu open showing the Auto-Answer checkbox and the Perform Actions using options Web Phone and Phone](/images/docs/live-calls-settings.png)
</Frame>

## Listen, Coach, or Join a call

Find the call in the list, then select one of the **Supervisor Actions**.

| Action | What it does | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| **Listen** | You hear the call. Neither the member nor the caller hears you. | Check call quality or follow a new member's first calls |
| **Coach** | You talk to the member during the call. The caller does not hear you. | Guide a member through a tricky conversation |
| **Join** | You become part of the conversation with the member and the caller. | Step in when the member needs direct help |

TalkChief confirms the action with a short message, for example "Listening in progress..." when you use **Phone**. The monitoring call then rings on the device you chose. To stop, hang up the monitoring call.

<Tip>
  Start with **Listen**. Move to **Coach** if the member needs a hint, and use **Join** only when the caller should hear you.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Monitoring rules differ by country. Make sure your members and callers are told about monitoring where the law requires it.
</Warning>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see all calls?">
    Supervisors see only the live calls of their own team. Admins see every live call in the company.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Nothing rang when I selected Listen. What should I check?">
    Open **Settings** on the **Live Calls** page and check **Perform Actions using:**. If you chose **Web Phone**, keep the TalkChief tab open and the **Web Phone** connected. If you chose **Phone**, make sure your TalkChief app or desk phone is signed in.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does the message 'Live calls could not be loaded' mean?">
    TalkChief could not fetch the list of calls in progress. Wait a few seconds for the next refresh. If it continues, contact support.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
