## Key Capabilities

TalkChief's Contact Center covers the full lifecycle of a customer call, from the moment a call arrives to post-call reporting.

**Inbound**
- **Call Queues** — hold callers in organized queues with custom hold music and position announcements.
- **IVR** — greet callers with self-service menus and route them automatically based on their input.
- **Skill-Based Routing** — match callers to agents with the right skills, language, or product expertise.
- **Queue Callback** — let callers request a callback rather than waiting on hold, reducing abandonment rates.

**Outbound**
- **Power Dialer** — a human-operated planned calling workflow that lets agents work through contact lists efficiently. Each call is manually initiated by the agent, keeping you in full control.

**Supervision**
- **Live Monitoring** — supervisors can silently listen to any active call without the agent or caller being aware.
- **Call Whispering** — coach agents mid-call by speaking to them privately without the customer hearing.
- **Call Recording** — record calls on a per-queue or per-agent basis, with configurable retention policies.

**Analytics**
- **Live Feed Dashboard** — see active calls, queue lengths, and agent statuses in real time.
- **Call Reports** — review historical call data, handle times, and outcomes.
- **Interactive BI Dashboards** — explore trends, filter by team or queue, and share insights across your organization.
