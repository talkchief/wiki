The TalkChief contact center helps teams share incoming calls, watch calls as they happen, and review performance afterward. It is built into the same workspace as your business phone. This page is for admins and supervisors who plan how their team handles calls.

<Note>
  Admins set up queues and teams. Supervisors can monitor the live calls of their own team. Agents work in a separate agent workspace. The number of queues depends on your plan, and AI features need the **Growth** or **Professional** plan. See [Plans and pricing](/plans-and-pricing).
</Note>

## Inbound calls

- **Queues**: hold callers with music and optional position announcements until an agent is free. Find them in **Team** > **Queues**.
- **Ring strategies**: choose how agents ring, for example **Ring All**, **Round Robin**, or **Least Number of Calls**.
- **Ring order**: drag agents in **Manage Agents** to set who rings first.
- **Failover**: send calls to a fallback call flow when no agent is available.
- **Call flows**: route a number to a queue, a team, or an **AI Agent** from the number's **Callflow**.

## Supervision

- **Live Calls**: see every call in progress, refreshed every few seconds, in **Calls** > **Live Calls**.
- **Listen**, **Coach**, and **Join**: monitor a call silently, talk only to the member, or join the conversation.
- **Escalations**: with AI transcription, TalkChief flags calls that may need a supervisor in **Insights** > **Escalations**.

## Agent workspace

Agents sign in to their own workspace. Their **Dashboard** shows whether they are **Available** or **On break**, with **Take break** and **Resume work** buttons. Their **Queues** page lists the queues they belong to.

## Insights

- **Dashboards** and **Reports** in the **Insights** group show call volume, queue outcomes, wait times, and agent activity.
- **Call search** finds calls by what was said, once AI transcription is on.

## Outbound calls

Members place calls from the **Web Phone** or the TalkChief apps. For automated outbound calling, AI voice agents can run campaigns from [agents.talkchief.io](https://agents.talkchief.io). Enterprise companies can request the [Power Dialer](/contact-center/power-dialer) add-on.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a special plan for queues?">
    Queues are available in the admin workspace. Each plan has its own queue limit. If you reach it, upgrade in **Settings** > **Plans**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can supervisors monitor every call?">
    Supervisors see the live calls of their own team. Admins see every live call in the company.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
