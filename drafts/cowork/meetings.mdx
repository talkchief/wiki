TalkChief Meetings are video meetings you start and join from CoWork. Colleagues join from CoWork, and guests join from any browser with a link. No account is needed for guests.

<Note>
  Meetings are part of the **Professional** plan. See [What each plan includes](/cowork/overview#what-each-plan-includes).
</Note>

## Start a meeting

You can start a meeting in three ways:

- **From a channel or group message:** select the call button in the conversation header. The meeting appears in the conversation as a card with a **Join** button.
- **With the `/meet` command:** type `/meet` in a channel or group message to start the meeting for that conversation, or join it if one is already running. In a direct message, `/meet` starts an instant meeting with the other person.
- **With Create meeting:** type `/meet new`, or use **Create meeting**, to give the meeting a title, choose to start now or schedule it for later, and invite people.

## Join a meeting

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="CoWork members">

On the join screen, check your name, then turn your microphone and camera on or off and choose a background before you enter. Select **Join meeting** to go in.

## Invite people

Select **More**, then **Invite people**, during a meeting. In CoWork, select **Invite** on the meeting card or in the **Meetings** panel.

<Frame>
  ![The Invite people dialog: search your workspace to invite members, copy the meeting link, or share the invitation](/images/docs/meetings-invite-people.png)
</Frame>

- **Invite workspace members:** search for a colleague and invite them. They get a CoWork message with the meeting card, and CoWork rings them while the meeting is running.
- **Copy the meeting link:** paste it anywhere to share it.
- **Invite people by email:** type or paste email addresses. Each person gets an email with a **Join meeting** link and a calendar invitation they can accept. Adding anyone by email allows guests in that meeting, and the guest option is locked on.

## In the meeting

<Frame>
  ![The meeting room with the meeting title and timer on top, everyone in a side strip, and the control bar at the bottom](/images/docs/meetings-room.png)
</Frame>

The bar at the bottom has these controls:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Mute** | Turns your microphone off and on. |
| **Start Video** | Turns your camera off and on. |
| **Share** | Shares your screen or a window. |
| **Chat** | Opens the meeting chat. |
| **Raise Hand** | Lets the host and others know you want to speak. |
| **React** | Sends a quick reaction. |
| **Participants** | Shows who is in the meeting. The host is marked **M**. |
| **View** | Changes how people are arranged on screen. |
| **More** | Opens **Invite people**, **Select Background**, and other options. |
| **Leave** | Leaves the meeting. |

### Share your screen

Select **Share** and choose what to share. Everyone else hears a short chime when you start. While you share, **Share** becomes a red **Stop Share**.

<Frame>
  ![What a member sees while someone shares their screen](/images/docs/meetings-someone-sharing.png)
</Frame>

In the CoWork desktop app on Windows, you can choose to share your computer's sound too. A note on screen tells you whether your share includes sound, and how to turn it on if it doesn't.

<Frame>
  ![The person sharing sees a red Stop Share button and a note saying whether the share includes sound](/images/docs/meetings-share-sound-note.png)
</Frame>

### Change your background

Select **More**, then **Select Background**. Choose blur or a background image and check the live preview. If CoWork can't use your camera, the tab says **No camera access** and offers **Grant camera access**.

<Frame>
  ![The background picker with blur and image choices and a live preview](/images/docs/meetings-backgrounds.png)
</Frame>

## Host controls

The person who created the meeting is its host and the only moderator. Everyone else is a participant. Nobody can remove other people from a meeting.

When the host selects **Leave**, they can leave, or choose **End meeting for all**. Members only see **Leave**.

<Frame>
  ![The Leave menu for the host with Leave and End meeting for all](/images/docs/meetings-leave-menu.png)
</Frame>

When the host ends the meeting, everyone sees **Meeting ended**.
