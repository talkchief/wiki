The Knowledge library holds the approved documents your agents can search during a call. You upload each document once for the whole workspace. Then you choose which inbound agents and campaigns may use it.

<Note>
Limits: PDF, DOCX, or TXT files up to **3 MB** each. A workspace holds up to **15** library documents. Each campaign can also have up to **2** private documents.
</Note>

## Upload a document

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Knowledge">
    In the agents workspace sidebar, under **Library**, click **Knowledge**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the file">
    Click **Add document**, then **Choose a file or drop it here**. Pick one PDF, DOCX, or TXT file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for indexing">
    The document appears in the **Reference library** with the status **Preparing**. When it is ready, the status changes to **Smart search ready** or **Keyword search ready**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  ![The Knowledge page Reference library table with three fake documents, their status badges, and the 3 of 15 documents counter.](/images/docs/knowledge-library.png)
</Frame>

A new document stays private until you grant it to an agent or select it in a campaign.

### Document status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Preparing** | TalkChief is reading and indexing the file |
| **Smart search ready** | The agent can find answers by meaning |
| **Keyword search ready** | The agent can find answers by matching words. Open the **⋯** menu and click **Retry smart search** to try full indexing again |
| **Needs attention** | Indexing failed. Check the error under the document name, fix the file, and upload it again |

## Help the agent pick the right document

Click **Manage access** on a document to edit its details. Clear details help the agent choose the right source.

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| **Display name** | Price list 2026 |
| **Language** | English |
| **What this document contains** | Treatment prices, payment plans, and insurance rules |
| **Other names callers may use** | price sheet, fees (up to 10, comma-separated) |
| **Topics** | pricing, insurance (up to 10, comma-separated) |

Click **Save document details**.

## Grant a document to an inbound agent

You can grant access from either place:

- **From the library**: click **Manage access** on the document. Under **Inbound agent access**, turn on the switch for each agent, then click **Done**.
- **From the agent**: open the agent, go to **Knowledge**, and turn on the switch next to each document. The status changes from **Not allowed** to **Allowed**.

Access changes apply to the agent's next search. You do not need to click **Save changes**.

<Frame>
  ![The agent's Knowledge section showing the Knowledge access card with two documents Allowed and one Not allowed.](/images/docs/agent-knowledge-access.png)
</Frame>

<Tip>
Each agent that uses knowledge needs one of its 25 voice action slots for knowledge search. See [Voice actions](/ai-agents/tools-and-integrations#voice-actions-limit).
</Tip>

## Add knowledge to a campaign

Outbound campaigns choose their own documents. Open the campaign, then go to **Knowledge & actions**.

- To use a library document, pick it in **Choose workspace knowledge…** and click **Add knowledge**.
- To add a file only this campaign can use, click **Upload campaign document**. Each campaign can hold 2 private documents.

Private campaign documents never appear in other campaigns or agents.

## Delete a document

Open the **⋯** menu on the document and click **Delete…**, or click **Delete document** in **Manage access**. Every agent and campaign loses access to it.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What should I upload?">
    Upload approved, current material: policies, service guides, price lists, opening hours, and processes. Short, well-structured documents work best.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I turn on access for a document?">
    The document is still preparing or needs attention. Wait for a ready status, or fix and re-upload the file.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can callers make the agent ignore its instructions with document text?">
    No. The agent treats document content as reference material only. It cannot change the agent's instructions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I have more than 15 documents. What should I do?">
    Combine related short files into one document, up to 3 MB. Delete documents you no longer use.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
