TalkChief AI Voice Agents answer and place phone calls for your business. An agent greets the caller, follows your instructions, looks up answers in your documents, runs approved actions, and transfers to a person when needed. Every call gets a transcript and a charge in **Call reports**.

<Note>
You need a TalkChief admin or supervisor account to open AI Voice Agents. Calls are charged to your TalkChief wallet, which must hold at least $10.
</Note>

## Open AI Voice Agents

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to TalkChief">
    Sign in to the TalkChief web app with an admin or supervisor account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the agents workspace">
    In the sidebar, under calls, click **AI Voice Agents**. The agents workspace opens in a new tab at agents.talkchief.io.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Inbound agents and outbound campaigns

The agents workspace has two ways to work.

| | Inbound agents | Outbound campaigns |
|---|---|---|
| What it does | Answers calls that reach your numbers | Calls a list of leads for you |
| Where you build it | **Inbound agents** | **Campaigns** |
| Who it sounds like | The agent's own persona and voice | A reusable caller profile |
| What it says | The agent's objective and greeting | The campaign's purpose, first line, and Goal playbook |
| How calls arrive | A call flow step sends calls to the agent | The campaign dials leads inside a calling window |

Library items are shared. You set up a document, email sender, tool, or transfer destination once. Then you grant it to each inbound agent or campaign that needs it.

## Pricing

AI voice agent calls cost **$0.15 per connected minute**, or the usage-based cost of the call if that is higher. The charge follows the call's connected AI time.

- Failed, busy, and unanswered attempts have no per-minute charge.
- When the agent transfers a caller to a person, charging stops at the transfer.
- Your wallet must hold at least **$10** before the agent takes or places a call.

You see each call's charge in **Call reports**. See [Plans and pricing](/plans-and-pricing) for wallet top-ups.

## What agents can and cannot do

Agents can:

- Speak English, Arabic, or Hebrew, or detect the caller's language.
- Search documents you grant them.
- Call your business systems through Business APIs and MCP connectors.
- Send email from your workspace sender.
- Transfer to approved people and queues, end calls, send keypad tones, and record do-not-call requests.

<Warning>
Agents do not record call audio. Call reports keep the written transcript only. Agents also do not run phone menus (IVR); build menus in your [call flow](/phone/call-flows) and route a menu option to the agent.
</Warning>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How many agents can I create?">
    Your plan sets an AI agent limit. The **Inbound agents** page shows how many you use, for example "2 of 3 AI agents". To get more, delete an agent you no longer need or ask TalkChief support to raise the limit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do changes need a publish step?">
    No. Click **Save changes** and new calls use the updated settings. **Publish** marks an inbound agent as ready after all its checks pass; a draft agent still answers the calls your call flow sends to it. Outbound caller profiles have a **Publish caller profile** step before their first campaign.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can the agent say it is an AI?">
    Yes. For inbound agents, turn on **Disclose AI when asked**. In outbound campaigns the agent always answers truthfully if a lead asks directly.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
