A campaign calls a list of leads for you. The campaign holds the message, rules, audience, documents, and tools. A reusable **caller profile** supplies the identity, voice, and phone line.

<Note>
You need a TalkChief phone user for the caller profile and at least $10 in your wallet. Each campaign places up to 5 calls at a time.
</Note>

## How a campaign works

| Part | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Caller profile** | Who calls: name, introduction, role, language, voice, tone, and phone line. Reusable across campaigns |
| **Campaign setup** | What the calls are for: purpose, first spoken line, Goal playbook, guidelines, and rules |
| **Audience & pacing** | Who to call and when: leads, calling window, concurrency, retries, and do-not-call |
| **Knowledge & actions** | What the agent can use: documents, tools, and transfer destinations |
| **Test & launch** | Readiness checks, a rehearsal call, and launch |
| **Activity & results** | Live calls, outcomes, charges, and configuration history |

## Step 1: Create the campaign

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a campaign">
    In the agents workspace sidebar, click **Campaigns**, then **New campaign**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and pick a goal">
    Enter a **Campaign name**. Under **What should these calls achieve?**, choose **Sell something**, **Collect information**, or **Book an appointment**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who calls">
    Under **Who should make these calls?**, choose **Create a caller profile** or **Use an existing profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up a new caller profile">
    Enter a **Caller profile name**, the **TalkChief email** and **TalkChief password** of the phone user it calls from, and **Introduces itself as**. TalkChief verifies the account and connects the phone. If verification fails, nothing is created.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create">
    Click **Create campaign**. The campaign opens on **Campaign setup**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  ![The Create a campaign dialog with a campaign name, the Book an appointment goal selected, and Create a caller profile chosen.](/images/docs/create-campaign-dialog.png)
</Frame>

<Tip>
One running or paused campaign holds a caller profile at a time. Create a second profile if you need two campaigns live at once.
</Tip>

## Step 2: Write the call plan

Open **Campaign setup**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Call plan">
    Fill in **Purpose / objective** and **First spoken line**. The first line should say who is calling and why.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Goal playbook">
    Answer the required questions for your goal. For example, **Book an appointment** asks what is booked, how long it lasts, the times you can offer, and what counts as booked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Instructions and absolute boundaries">
    Add **General guidelines**, **Never do** rules, and **Additional context** as needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Conversation behavior">
    Choose **Allow brief small talk**, **Allow the agent to end calls**, and **Allow phone-menu keypresses**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save campaign**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Two rules always apply. The agent honors verbal opt-outs and ends the call. If a lead asks directly, the agent says it is an AI.

## Step 3: Finish and publish the caller profile

The **Reusable caller profile** card on the same page controls how the caller sounds. It is separate from the campaign's instructions.

1. Check **Profile name**, **Introduces itself as**, and **Role**.
2. Choose the language, dialect, and **Voice**. Use **Voice sample** to listen.
3. Set **Tone**, **Pace**, **Response consistency**, **Let leads interrupt**, and **Listening pause**.
4. Click **Save caller profile**.
5. Check that **Outbound phone connection** shows a connected phone with outbound calls enabled.
6. Click **Publish caller profile**.

<Warning>
You cannot rehearse or launch until the caller profile is published and its outbound phone is connected.
</Warning>

## Step 4: Import leads

Open **Audience & pacing**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the file">
    Under **Import leads**, click **Choose or drop a lead CSV**. Files can be up to 10 MB. Click **Download template** for an example.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map the columns">
    Map **Phone number** (required). **Contact name**, **Company**, and **Lead timezone** are optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import">
    Check the preview and click **Import**. The result shows how many leads were added and why any rows were skipped.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Phone numbers must be in international format, such as +14155550123, or a TalkChief extension. Duplicates and numbers on your do-not-call list are skipped.

## Step 5: Set the calling window and pacing

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Calling days | Days of the week the campaign may call |
| **Start time** and **End time** | The daily calling window |
| **Campaign timezone** | The timezone for the window, for example `Asia/Riyadh` |
| **Respect each lead's timezone** | Applies the window in each lead's local time when a timezone is mapped |
| **Concurrent calls** | 1 to 5 calls at a time |
| **Retry attempts** | 0 to 10 retries after a retryable outcome, such as no answer |
| **Retry gap (minutes)** | Minimum wait between attempts on the same lead |
| **Check workspace do-not-call list before every dial** | Skips numbers on your do-not-call list |

The campaign only dials inside the window, for leads not on the do-not-call list, with attempts left.

## Step 6: Add knowledge and actions

Open **Knowledge & actions** to choose what this campaign can use.

- **Knowledge**: add library documents, or upload up to 2 private campaign documents.
- **Live transfer directory**: add approved transfer destinations.
- **Actions**: attach workspace Business APIs, email, or MCP tools, or click **Add a tool** to create a private API for this campaign.

The **Voice actions** meter applies here too, with a limit of 25. See [Tools and integrations](/ai-agents/tools-and-integrations).

## Step 7: Rehearse and launch

Open **Test & launch**. The checklist shows each requirement:

- **Call plan is specific**: a campaign purpose and an opening line.
- **Goal playbook is complete**.
- **Caller profile is ready** and **Caller profile is published**.
- **Outbound phone is connected**, with outbound calls enabled.
- **Callable audience is loaded**: at least one lead to call.
- **Enabled resources are ready**, **Caller profile is available**, and **All edits are saved**.

Click **Fix** next to any failed item to jump to the setting.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Place a rehearsal">
    Under **Rehearsal call**, enter your **Test number or extension** and an optional **Test contact name**. Click **Place rehearsal**. The call uses the saved campaign, but does not touch your leads.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the rehearsal">
    Click **Open rehearsal report**. Rehearsal calls carry the **Rehearsal** label in **Call reports**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch">
    When every check passes, click **Launch campaign**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Monitor and control the campaign

The header shows progress and the lead outcome bar. Lead states are:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Waiting to be called |
| **Dialling** | Being called now |
| **Completed** | Finished |
| **Failed** | The call attempt did not succeed |
| **Suppressed** | Skipped because of do-not-call or an invalid number |

**Activity & results** shows the live call transcript, each call's outcome, duration, and cost, and the configuration history. Click **Inspect** to open a call in **Call reports**.

- **Pause safely** stops new calls. Calls in progress can finish. Pause to edit the campaign.
- **Stop campaign** cancels the remaining leads. You cannot undo this.
- The campaign status changes to **Completed** when every lead is processed.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I edit a running campaign?">
    No. Click **Pause safely** or **Pause to edit**, make your changes, save, and launch again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the agent detect voicemail?">
    No. Automated answer detection is not available. If a mailbox answers, the call is treated as answered.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I reuse a caller profile?">
    Yes. Choose **Use an existing profile** when you create the next campaign. The profile stays available after a campaign ends or is deleted.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How much does a campaign cost?">
    Each connected call costs $0.15 per minute, or the usage-based cost if higher. Unanswered and busy attempts have no per-minute charge.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
