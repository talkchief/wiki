TalkChief Business Phone is the calling side of your workspace. You buy numbers, decide what happens when someone calls them, and make and take calls in your browser. Admins set it up. Supervisors and agents use it every day.

## Where to find it

Everything sits in the left sidebar of the TalkChief web app. Admins and supervisors see five groups.

| Sidebar group | Items for Business Phone |
|---|---|
| **Workspace** | **Dashboard** |
| **Calls** | **Numbers**, **Live Calls**, **Voicemail**, **AI Voice Agents**, **Call Flow** |
| **Team** | **Teams & Members**, **Queues** |
| **Insights** | **Dashboards**, **Reports** |
| **Configuration** | **Media**, **Integrations**, **Settings** |

**Call Flow** is a menu. Select it and pick a number to open that number's call flow. **AI Voice Agents** opens in a new tab.

<Note>
  Members with the agent role see a shorter sidebar: **Dashboard** and **Queues** under **Workspace**, and **Settings** under **Account**. See [Agent workspace](/contact-center/agent-workspace).
</Note>

## Who does what

| Role | What they do in Business Phone |
|---|---|
| Admin | Buys and releases numbers, edits call flows, manages teams, members, queues, and media. |
| Supervisor | Uses the same admin area to work with their teams. Some actions, such as buying numbers, are for admins only. |
| Agent | Makes and takes calls in the Web Phone and sees their queues, extension, and break status. |

## Set up your phone system

<Steps>
  <Step title="Buy a number">
    Go to **Calls** → **Numbers** and select **Buy number**. See [Numbers and caller ID](/phone/numbers-and-caller-id).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create teams and invite members">
    Go to **Team** → **Teams & Members** to create teams and invite members. See [Teams and members](/contact-center/teams-and-members).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your audio">
    Go to **Configuration** → **Media** to upload greetings and hold music. See [Working hours and media](/phone/working-hours-and-media).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the call flow">
    Open **Calls** → **Call Flow**, pick your number, and add steps. See [Call flows](/phone/call-flows).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    Call your number and check that it rings the right people. Answer in the [Web Phone](/phone/web-phone).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to install anything to make calls?">
    No. The Web Phone runs in your browser on every page of the app. Desktop apps are also available from the agent dashboard.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see Numbers or Call Flow in my sidebar?">
    You're signed in with the agent role. Agents see **Dashboard**, **Queues**, and **Settings** only. Ask an admin if you need more access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I give a caller to an AI voice agent?">
    Yes. Add the **AI Agent** step to a call flow. See [Add an AI agent to a call flow](/ai-agents/add-to-call-flow).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
