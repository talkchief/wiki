TalkChief's Business Phone system gives your team a fully cloud-based voice platform — no desk phones required. Whether you're a growing startup handling your first support queue or an enterprise coordinating agents across multiple regions, TalkChief centralises inbound and outbound calling, intelligent routing, and real-time collaboration into a single, manageable workspace.

## Supported Apps

TalkChief's Business Phone works across every major platform so your team can take and make calls from wherever they work best:

- **Windows** — full-featured desktop application
- **macOS** — native desktop application
- **iOS** — mobile app for iPhone and iPad
- **Android** — mobile app for phones and tablets
- **Browser** — no installation needed; works in modern web browsers

<Note>
  Specific capabilities — such as desktop notifications, ring on speakers, or parallel call handling — may vary depending on your plan and assigned user roles. Check your account settings or contact support to confirm which features are available for your team.
</Note>

## Global Voice Coverage

TalkChief provides voice coverage in more than 90 countries, so your team can establish a local presence virtually anywhere your customers are. For businesses operating in the Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) region and Saudi Arabia (KSA), TalkChief maintains direct connectivity with in-country infrastructure, delivering lower latency and regulatory compliance. In Israel, TalkChief operates through partnerships with established local providers to ensure reliable, compliant call termination and origination.
