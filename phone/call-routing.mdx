Call routing decides who rings when a call reaches one of your numbers. In TalkChief you combine a few building blocks: call flows, teams, queues, working hours, and each member's call forwarding.

## How a call is routed

1. The call reaches your number and starts that number's [call flow](/phone/call-flows).
2. A **Time Condition** step can check the team's working hours and pick the **Open** or **Closed** branch.
3. An **IVR** step can let the caller choose a department.
4. The call then rings a **Team**, a **Member**, an **AI Agent**, or a **Queue**, or it goes to **Voicemail** or **Call Forward**.
5. If a member doesn't answer, their own call forwarding settings can send the call to their mobile.

## Ring a team

The **Team** step in a call flow rings a group of members. In its settings you choose:

| Setting | Options |
|---|---|
| **Ring strategy** | **In order** rings one member at a time. **Simultaneously** rings everyone at once. |
| **Ring each member for** / **Keep ringing for** | 15–60 seconds. |
| **Repeat** | 1–15 rounds. |
| **Media** | Audio to play while the call rings. |

Each team also has a **Team Extension**. Calling that extension reaches all team members.

## Send calls to a queue

Queues hold callers until an agent is free. You create them in **Team** → **Queues** and add them to a flow with the **Queue** step.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the queue">
    Go to **Team** → **Queues** and select **+ New Queue**. Under **Queue Details**, enter the **Queue Name** and **Extension Number**. Set the **Queue timeout (Max wait time)**, **Queue SLA Threshold**, **Time Zone**, and hold **Media**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how agents ring">
    Under **Agent Details**, pick a **Ring Strategy**, set the **Agent Wrap-up time**, and set **Ring each agent for**. Select **Create Queue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add agents">
    On the queue card, open **Actions** and select **Manage Agents**. Choose the members who take calls from this queue, drag them to set the **Ring order**, then save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the queue to a call flow">
    Open the number's call flow and add a **Queue** step. See [Call flows](/phone/call-flows).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Available ring strategies: **Ring All**, **Round Robin**, **In Order**, **Least Talk Time**, **Least Number of Calls**, **By Agent Skill & Order**, **Random**, and **Ring Progressively**.

You can also turn on **Enable Position Announcement** to tell callers their place in line. Set the **Announcement Interval** and **Announcement Language**.

For queue reporting and live monitoring, see [Queues and routing](/contact-center/queues-and-routing).

### Queue failover

On the queue card, open **Actions** and select **Failover**. This shows the call flow of the queue's failover number, where calls go when no agent is available. If none is set, you see **No failover number is configured for this queue.**

## Route by working hours

Each team has its own **Working Hours** on its **Settings** tab. A **Time Condition** step in the call flow uses them. During working hours the call takes the **Open** branch. At other times it takes the **Closed** branch. See [Working hours and media](/phone/working-hours-and-media).

## Forward a member's calls

Every member can forward calls to an outside number when they can't answer.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the member's call forward">
    Go to **Team** → **Teams & Members** and open the **Members** tab. In the **Call Forward** column, select the member's setting. You can also open the member's **Settings** page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Turn on **Enable call forward**. Choose the **Country** and enter the **Phone Number**, 9–13 digits, with the country code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the options">
    Turn on any of these options, then save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Failover** | Forwards calls only when all the member's devices are offline. |
| **Bypass users phones** | Forwards right away without ringing the member's devices. |
| **Direct calls only** | Forwards only calls made straight to the member, not calls from a ring group or queue. |
| **Require Key press** | The member must press a key to accept the forwarded call. |

<Tip>
  Use **Require Key press** when forwarding to a mobile. It stops the mobile's own voicemail from taking the call.
</Tip>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference between a team and a queue?">
    A **Team** step rings members right away and stops after the set rounds. A **Queue** keeps callers waiting with hold audio until an agent is free, and it tracks wait times and SLA.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I forward a whole number to my mobile?">
    Yes. Add a **Call Forward** step to the number's call flow and enter your mobile number.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
