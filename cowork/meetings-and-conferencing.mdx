TalkChief CoWork has two kinds of calls. **CoWork calls** are group calls with colleagues inside a channel or message. **Phone calls** use your TalkChief business phone to reach any number or extension, right inside CoWork.

| | CoWork calls | Phone calls |
|---|---|---|
| Who you call | Colleagues in CoWork | Any phone number or extension |
| Where you start | **Start call** in a channel or message | **Dialer**, **Address Book**, or a direct message |
| Extras | Screen sharing, **Add members**, **Copy call link** | Hold, transfer, keypad, conference up to 5 people |

## Start a group call

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the conversation">
    Open the channel or group message you want to call from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the call">
    Select **Start call** in the conversation header. If a call is already running there, the button says **Join call**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring people in">
    People in a direct or group message get a ringing notification. In a channel, members see the call and can select **Join**. To invite someone else, select **Add members** in the call widget.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share a link">
    Select **Copy call link** to copy a link that opens the conversation and joins the running call.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When you invite a third person to a call in a direct message, CoWork moves the call to a new group message with everyone in it.

## Share your screen

During a CoWork call, select the screen share button in the call widget and choose what to share. Select it again to stop sharing.

## After a call

When a call ends, its post in the conversation shows **Call ended**, when it ended, how long it lasted, and who joined.

## Make a phone call

CoWork includes your TalkChief business phone.

- Select **Dialer** in the conversation header to open the keypad and your **Recent Calls**. Enter a number and select **Call**.
- Select **Address Book** in the header to find a contact and call them. You can add contacts there, and share them with your team.
- In a direct message with a colleague, the call button rings their TalkChief extension. If CoWork can't find their extension, it starts a CoWork call instead.

Incoming phone calls ring in CoWork too, so you can answer them in the app.

### During a phone call

The call bar shows **Mute**, **Hold**, **Add**, **Transfer**, and **Keypad**, and **End** to hang up. **Transfer** hands the call to a saved contact or any number you type.

  ![CoWork desktop phone call in a direct message, with the Add to call list of colleagues and AI agents and the call bar with Mute, Hold, Add, Transfer, Keypad and End](/images/docs/cowork-desktop-call.png)

### Turn a phone call into a conference

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add someone">
    On the call bar, select **Add** and pick a contact, or type a number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage the conference">
    Select **People** to open **Participants**. From there you can **Mute all**, lock the call so no one else can join, and add up to 5 people in total.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave or end">
    Select **Leave** to drop out while the others keep talking. To hang up for everyone, open the menu next to **Leave** and select **End for everyone**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Phone conferences are recorded. A **REC** indicator shows on the call bar while a conference is running. Tell the people on the call, and check the recording rules that apply to you.
</Note>

## Escalated call alerts

CoWork does not summarize CoWork calls. TalkChief AI transcription works on your business phone calls. It can post escalated calls, with their AI summary, to a CoWork channel your team watches. See [Escalations](/ai/escalations#cowork-channels) and [Transcripts and summaries](/ai/transcripts-and-summaries).
