TalkChief Meetings are video meetings you create and join from CoWork. Colleagues join from CoWork on desktop or in the browser. Guests join from any browser with a link, with no account and no install.

<Note>
  Meetings are part of the **Professional** plan at no extra cost. See [What each plan includes](/cowork/overview#what-each-plan-includes).
</Note>

## Create a meeting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Create meeting">
    Open the **Meetings** panel in CoWork and select **Create meeting**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set it up">
    Enter a title and choose **Start now** or **Schedule**. Invite members from your workspace, and invite anyone else by email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the link">
    On **Meeting ready**, select **Copy link** or **Copy invitation** to share it, or **Join now** to go in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  ![The Create meeting dialog with the meeting title, Start now or Schedule, invitations for workspace members and by email, and the guest option](/images/docs/cowork-meetings-create.png)
</Frame>

<Frame>
  ![Meeting ready, with the guest link, Copy link, Copy invitation and Join now. The link shown is a placeholder](/images/docs/cowork-meetings-ready.png)
</Frame>

- **Workspace members** get the invitation and a reminder in CoWork.
- **People invited by email** get the link and a calendar invitation, and join as guests.
- **Recurring calendar meetings** keep the same link for every occurrence.

## Join a meeting

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="CoWork members">
    Join from CoWork on desktop or in the browser. You join with your CoWork name. On the join screen, check your microphone, camera and background, then select **Join meeting**.

    <Frame>
      ![The join screen before entering a meeting, with your name and avatar, the Join meeting button, and buttons for microphone, camera, invite, background, and settings](/images/docs/cowork-meetings-join-screen.png)
    </Frame>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Guests">
    Guests open the guest link in a browser. They select **Open in TalkChief Cowork**, or type a name and select **Join in browser**. Guests wait in the lobby until someone in the meeting lets them in.

    <Frame>
      ![The guest page with Open in TalkChief Cowork, or a name field and Join in browser. The link is a placeholder](/images/docs/cowork-meetings-guest-page.png)
    </Frame>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Invite people during a meeting

Select **More**, then **Invite people**. Search your workspace to invite members, copy the meeting link, or share the invitation.

<Frame>
  ![The Invite people dialog: search your workspace to invite members, copy the meeting link, or share the invitation](/images/docs/cowork-meetings-invite-people.png)
</Frame>

## In the meeting

Meetings include screen sharing with sound, chat, raise hand, reactions, backgrounds and blur, and tile view.

<Frame>
  ![The meeting room with the meeting title and timer on top, everyone in a side strip, and the control bar at the bottom](/images/docs/cowork-meetings-room.png)
</Frame>

### Share your screen

Select **Share** and choose what to share. Everyone else hears a short chime when you start. While you share, the button becomes a red **Stop Share**, and a note tells you whether the share includes sound.

<Frame>
  ![What a member sees while someone shares their screen](/images/docs/cowork-meetings-someone-sharing.png)
</Frame>

<Frame>
  ![The person sharing sees a red Stop Share button and a note saying whether the share includes sound](/images/docs/cowork-meetings-share-sound-note.png)
</Frame>

### Change your background

Select **More**, then **Select Background**. Choose blur or a background image and check the live preview. If CoWork can't use your camera, the tab says **No camera access** and offers **Grant camera access**.

<Frame>
  ![The background picker with blur and image choices and a live preview](/images/docs/cowork-meetings-backgrounds.png)
</Frame>

## Leave or end a meeting

Select **Leave** to leave. The host can also choose **End meeting for all**. Members only see **Leave**. When the host ends the meeting, everyone sees **Meeting ended**.
