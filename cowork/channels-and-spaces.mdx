TalkChief CoWork organizes your team's conversations into channels, direct messages, and threads. Everyone in your company works in one shared CoWork workspace. This page explains each way to talk and how to set up your first channels.

## Your workspace

Each company has one CoWork workspace. Everyone who signs in with your company's TalkChief account joins it. Only workspace admins can invite people to the workspace. See [CoWork overview](/cowork/overview) to sign in.

## Channels

A channel is a group conversation about one topic, team, or project. There are two kinds:

- **Public Channel**: anyone in the workspace can find it with **Browse channels** and join.
- **Private Channel**: only people who are added can see it.

Keep each channel focused on one topic. Some common examples:

- `support-escalations` for urgent customer issues
- `sales-team` for sales updates
- `announcements` for company news

## Direct and group messages

Direct messages are private conversations between two people. They are listed under **DIRECT MESSAGES** in the sidebar. To start one, select **+** at the top of the sidebar, then **Open a direct message**, and pick a colleague.

A group message is a private conversation with up to 9 people. To turn a direct message into a group message, select **Add Members** in the conversation header.

## Threads

Select **Reply** on any message to answer it in a thread. Threads keep a discussion together without filling the channel. Open **Threads** in the sidebar to see all threads you follow.

## Status

Your status tells teammates whether you can talk. Set it from your profile picture: **Online**, **Away**, **Do not disturb**, or **Offline**.

## Create your first channels

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open CoWork">
    Sign in at [cowork.talkchief.io](https://cowork.talkchief.io) or in the CoWork desktop or mobile app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a channel">
    Select **+** at the top of the sidebar, then **Create new channel**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the channel">
    In **Create a new channel**, enter a **Channel name** and choose **Public Channel** or **Private Channel**. Add a purpose so people know what the channel is for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Select **Create channel**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add people">
    Open the channel's **Members** list and add the colleagues who should be in it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Agree on a naming pattern before you create many channels, for example a `support-` prefix for support channels. Consistent names make channels easier to find.
</Tip>

## Post alerts to a channel

A channel can receive messages from other systems through an incoming webhook. TalkChief uses this to post AI-detected escalated calls to a channel your team watches. Create the webhook in CoWork under **Integrations** > **Incoming Webhooks** and point it at the channel, then connect it in TalkChief. See [Escalations](/ai/escalations#cowork-channels) for the full steps.

## Message history

CoWork keeps messages and files for 180 days. After that they are deleted permanently and can't be restored. Save anything you need to keep longer somewhere else.

<Tip>
  To talk live, start a call from any channel or message. See [CoWork calls and meetings](/cowork/meetings-and-conferencing).
</Tip>
