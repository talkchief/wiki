Instead of juggling separate apps for messaging and conferencing, Cowork lets you move from a text conversation to a live call in one click, keeping the full context of your discussion exactly where your team can find it.

## Why Cowork?

Business conversations don't exist in isolation — a decision made on a call shapes the work your team does in chat, and a thread in a channel often leads to a call. Cowork keeps that context intact by tying your calling history directly to the channels and threads where the work happens, so nothing gets lost between apps. When a call ends, AI-generated summaries can post automatically back to the originating channel, giving every team member — whether they joined the call or not — immediate visibility into what was decided. The result is fewer dropped handoffs, less context-switching, and a single source of truth for both your conversations and your calls.
