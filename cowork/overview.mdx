TalkChief CoWork is TalkChief's team collaboration app. It puts channels, direct messages, group calls with screen sharing, and your TalkChief business phone in one place. CoWork is a separate app from the TalkChief web app. You sign in with your TalkChief account.

CoWork is available on every plan. **Essential** and **Growth** get the CoWork desktop app for Windows and macOS with your business phone. **Professional** adds the full workspace with team chat and meetings, CoWork on the web, and the iOS and Android apps. See [What each plan includes](#what-each-plan-includes).

## What you can do in CoWork

- **Chat with your team** in public and private channels, direct messages, and group messages. Reply in threads to keep a topic together.
- **Start a group call** from a channel or message, add people while the call runs, and share your screen.
- **Use your business phone**: open the **Dialer** to call any number, keep an **Address Book** of contacts, and answer incoming phone calls in the same app.
- **Get escalation alerts**: AI transcription can post escalated calls to a CoWork channel, with the call summary when it is ready. See [Escalations](/ai/escalations#cowork-channels).
- **See dashboards**: admins and supervisors can open **Dashboards** in CoWork, with **Outbound**, **Inbound**, **Queues**, **Billing**, and **AI insights** tabs.

### Essential and Growth: the phone in the desktop app

On Essential and Growth, CoWork opens as a compact phone window on Windows and macOS. It has the **Dialer**, **Address Book**, **Recents**, and **Voicemail**, and it shows incoming and active calls. Team chat, channels, meetings, and chat notifications are not available, and colleagues can't start a chat with you.

If your company changes plan while you are signed in, CoWork switches to the matching layout within a couple of minutes. You don't need to sign out. Moving up to Professional turns on chat. Moving down to Essential or Growth turns it off and signs you out of the web and mobile apps. Nothing is deleted, and your channels and messages are back as before if you upgrade again.

### Professional: the full workspace

Professional members get channels, direct messages, group calls, and everything else on this page. In the desktop app you can switch between the compact phone window and the full workspace. CoWork also runs on the web and on iOS and Android.

## Who can use CoWork

CoWork is included in every TalkChief plan. A free trial includes what the trial plan includes. Each company has one CoWork workspace.

- A TalkChief admin creates the workspace the first time they sign in to CoWork.
- After that, other members of the company join the same workspace when they first sign in.
- Only workspace admins can invite people to the workspace.

## Sign in to CoWork

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open CoWork">
    Open the desktop app. On the Professional plan you can also go to [cowork.talkchief.io](https://cowork.talkchief.io) or open the mobile app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your workspace name">
    In **Workspace name**, enter your company's workspace name. If you don't know it, select **Dont know your workspace Name?** and use **Find workspace name** with your TalkChief email or username and password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your TalkChief login">
    Enter the email or username and password you use for TalkChief, then select **Log in**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the workspace (first admin only)">
    If your company has no workspace yet and you are a TalkChief admin, CoWork shows **Create your Cowork workspace**. Check your email and workspace name, then select **Create workspace**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your CoWork password is your TalkChief password. Change it, and manage two-factor authentication and sessions, in TalkChief, not in CoWork.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="&quot;CoWork in the browser is included with the Professional plan&quot;">
    Your company is on Essential or Growth. These plans use the CoWork desktop app only. Download **Windows · MSI** or **macOS** from the **TalkChief CoWork** card on your TalkChief dashboard, or ask your admin to upgrade to Professional.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="&quot;The mobile app is included with the Professional plan&quot;">
    The iOS and Android apps are part of Professional. Use the desktop app for calls, or ask your admin to upgrade.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I don't see channels or chat in CoWork">
    Team chat is part of the Professional plan. On Essential and Growth, CoWork shows only the phone: **Dialer**, **Address Book**, **Recents**, and **Voicemail**. See [What each plan includes](#what-each-plan-includes).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  CoWork keeps messages and files for 180 days. Older messages and files are deleted permanently and can't be restored.
</Note>
